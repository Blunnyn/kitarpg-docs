Step by step guides

Draw the world map

The map window (M) can show a picture of the whole world with one zone per map. Players see where they are, each zone's name and levels, and click a zone to list its monsters and what they drop. This guide adds a zone to the sample's island.

#1. Open the editor

Open KitARPG > World Map Editor. It opens the database's world map; the sample's is WorldMap in Samples/Starter/Data. For your own game, press New world map and set its Picture: any sprite of your world, painted or drawn. The sample's island is painted by Art/Tools/make_world_map.py.

#2. Draw a zone

  1. Drag on empty space of the picture. A rectangle zone appears and is picked.
  2. Press Add corner on the right as many times as you need. Each one splits the longest side.
  3. Drag the corners around the area. Here they go around the mountains.

Scroll to zoom, and drag with the middle button (or with Alt) to move around.

#3. Set it up

With the zone picked, on the right:

Field What it does
Name Shown on the zone. Empty uses its map's name.
Map The map it stands for. Players on that map see themselves in this zone, and its monsters are listed.
Color The fill of the zone.
Min Level, Max Level The levels shown under the name. At 0 they come from the map's monsters.
World Area How much ground the map's scene covers, so players show at the right spot in the zone. Press World area from the scene to read it.

#4. Try it

Press Play, enter the game and press M. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, click a zone to see its monsters, and a monster to see its drops. The search at the top finds zones and monsters by name.

A map's monsters come from its spawners: after adding one, press Read from scene on the map in the Game Database (World > Maps).
