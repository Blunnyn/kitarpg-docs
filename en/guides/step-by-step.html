Step by step guides

Each guide makes one thing from start to finish in the sample project, with a picture of every step and the result in the game. Follow them in order the first time: the quest uses the monster and the potion made before it.

Guide What you make What you learn
Make a potion A healing potion sold by the alchemist The Game Database, items, item effects, shops
Make a hat A hat that shows on every body, and a slime that sits on your head Gear looks, Try on, model variants, attached models
Make a monster A Moss Golem living in the forest The New monster assistant, loot, spawners
Make a skill Frost Wave, a cone of ice that slows Skill areas, effects, giving a skill to a class
Make a quest Golem Trouble, given by the town captain Objectives, rewards, quest options on NPCs
Draw the world map A new zone on the world map The World Map Editor

Before you start:

  • The sample is built (KitARPG > Starter > Build Scenes and Prefabs, or the button in Overview). See Setup wizard.
  • You can play in single player. See Quick start: single player.
  • GM commands are on in single player (Game Settings, Single Player Commands). The guides use them to test fast: type them in the chat. See GM commands.
Tip

Everything the guides make is a normal asset in your project. Delete it from the Game Database (Delete on its page) when you no longer need it.
