Step by step guides

Make a monster

This guide makes a Moss Golem from a 3D model, gives it loot and puts three of them in the forest.

#1. Start the assistant

  1. In Game Database, open World > Monsters.
  2. Press + New monster. The assistant opens on the right, in four steps: Model, Combat, Animations and Create.

#2. Model

  1. Drag the model into the field: an FBX or a prefab. This guide uses Art/3D/VoxelHero/Monsters/Golem.
  2. The line under it says Animated when the model has an Animator, or No Animator. Without one the monster still moves, hops, attacks on time and flashes when hit (the MonsterBody component does it), like the sample's slimes.
  3. Type the Display name: Moss Golem. The id under it, moss_golem, is made from it.

Press Next.

#3. Combat

Start from a monster copies everything another monster has. Leave it on Nothing and set:

Field Value
Level 6
Health 260
Damage 8 to 14
Move speed 2.4
Experience 60
Loot table GolemLoot
Respawn seconds 45

The rest keeps its default: melee range 1.4, 0.8 attacks per second, Aggressive.

Press Next. The Animations step only has something to pick for animated models: the idle, walk, hit, death and attack clips, and when the attack's hit lands. Press Next again.

#4. Create

The last step shows where the prefab goes and lists what will be made. Keep Render its bestiary icon on and press Create monster.

The kit makes the monster data, a prefab with everything a monster needs (network, movement, click area, health plate) and its icon, adds it to the database and opens it. Skills, phases, resistances and the rest are edited here.

#5. Put it in the world

Monsters live where a Monster Spawner keeps them.

  1. Open the Forest scene (Samples/Starter/Scenes).
  2. GameObject > Create Empty, name it Moss Golem Spawner, and move it to open ground on the NavMesh (the blue area when the AI Navigation overlay is on), away from the SpawnPoint.
  3. Add Component > Monster Spawner. Set Monster to Moss Golem, Count 3 and Radius 6.
  4. Save the scene.
The spawner in the forest Its inspector
  1. In the Game Database, open World > Maps > Forest and press Read from scene. The bestiary and the world map list the Moss Golem in the forest from now on.

#6. Try it

  1. Press Play and enter the game.
  2. Walk to the forest, or type /map forest.
  3. Type /god to watch the fight without dying, and find the golems.

/spawn moss_golem 3 brings three in front of you anywhere (they don't come back), and /killall 20 clears them.
