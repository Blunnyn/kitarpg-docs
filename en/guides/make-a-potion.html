Step by step guides

Make a potion

This guide makes a Greater Health Potion: it heals 150 at once, then keeps healing for 20 seconds, and Mira the alchemist sells it in town.

#1. Open the consumables

  1. Open the KitARPG window with KitARPG > Open KitARPG (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K) and go to Game Database.
  2. Under Items, click Items, then Consumables. The list shows every potion, scroll and book of the game.

#2. Make the item

Press + New consumable at the bottom of the list. The kit makes the asset next to the others, adds it to the database and opens it.

The page starts with In game: the tooltip as players will see it. It updates as you type.

#3. Fill it in

In General:

Field Value Why
Id greater_health_potion Saved with every copy players own. Set it now and don't change it later.
Display Name Greater Health Potion
Description Restores a lot of health, then keeps healing for a while. Bottom line of the tooltip.
Icon Any sprite The sample's potion icons are in Art/Sprites/Icons.
Rarity Magic Colors the name blue.
Max Stack 20 20 fit in one bag slot.
Sell Price 15 Shops ask four times this unless you set Buy Price.
Restore Health 150 Healed at once.

To rename the file too, use Show file and rename it in the Project window. The id stays.

#4. Add an effect

Effects add anything beyond healing: experience, levels, a skill, a status, a trip home.

  1. Open Effects and press +.
  2. Press Pick a type on the new line and pick Status.
  3. Set Status to Regeneration, Duration to 20 and Level to 1.

The tooltip now adds "Regeneration for 20s" and what it does. Every effect type is on Items.

#5. Sell it at a shop

  1. In the Game Database, open Items > Shops and pick Alchemist.
  2. Open Items, press + at the bottom and pick the Greater Health Potion.
  3. Set Price to 60. Leave Stock at 0 for an endless supply.

Mira uses this shop, so nothing else is needed. To sell from a new NPC, see NPC shops.

#6. Try it

  1. Open the Title scene and press Play. Enter the game in single player.
  2. Walk to Mira, the alchemist in the middle of town, and click her. The potion is last in her list.
  1. Buy one, or type /item greater_health_potion 5 in the chat.
  2. Use it from the bag (double click) or drag it to the hotbar and press its key.

A potion that only heals can't be used at full health. This one has Regeneration too, so it can.
