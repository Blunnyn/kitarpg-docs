Step by step guides

Make a hat

This guide makes two head pieces: a Festival Hat that takes the place of the head's gear on every body, and a Slime Buddy, a monster model that sits on the character's head without being rigged.

#How gear shows on a character

Player characters are made of regions: head, hair, chest, hands, legs, feet and back. A piece of gear says how it shows with Look:

Look What it does For
Replace Its skinned mesh takes the region's place. Rigged gear made for your bodies' skeleton.
Add Its skinned mesh is drawn over the region, which stays. Rigged layers: a scarf, a belt.
Attach Its model hangs from a bone as it is. Any model that isn't rigged: props, monsters, crowns from the Asset Store.
None Nothing shows. Rings, or stats-only gear.

More on regions and naming on Player models.

#Part 1: a rigged hat

#1. Make the item

  1. In Game Database, open Items > Items > Hats.
  2. Press + New hat. The new item is already in the Head slot.
  3. Set Id festival_hat, Display Name Festival Hat and a Description.
  4. Set Model to the hat's FBX. The sample's hats are in Art/3D/VoxelHero/Gear/Hats; this guide uses JesterHat.

#2. Set how it looks

Scroll to Look and wear:

  • Look: Replace.
  • Piece: Auto picks the mesh named after the region (like Knight_Head) or the only one. If your model names its meshes otherwise, pick the right one from the list.
  • Hides: regions not drawn while it is worn. Head gear hides the hair by itself; turn on Keeps Hair for crowns and headbands.
  • Model Variants: a model for another body. The panda's head is bigger, so add a variant with Model Id voxel_panda and Model JesterHat_Panda.

#3. Check it in Try on

At the bottom of the page, Try on shows the piece on a player model with the game's own code. Pick a model in the list, turn on Close up, drag to turn and scroll to zoom. If it can't show on that model, a message says why, for example a missing mesh name.

Make its icon with KitARPG > Render Missing Item Icons: it photographs every item that has a model and no icon.

#Part 2: a model that isn't rigged

#1. Make the item

Make another hat as above: Id slime_buddy, Display Name Slime Buddy, Model Art/3D/VoxelHero/Monsters/Slime.

#2. Attach it

In Look and wear:

Field Value What it does
Look Attach Hangs the model from a bone.
Keeps Hair On The slime sits on the hair.
Attach Bone Its region's bone The head, for a hat. Pick any humanoid bone instead, like a hand or the spine.
Attach Position Y 0.235 Meters from the bone. Up, here.
Attach Scale 0.5, 0.5, 0.5 Half size.

Attach Both Sides puts a mirrored copy on the other side, for gloves, boots and pauldrons.

Watch Try on while you change the numbers: it updates at once.

#3. Try them

  1. Press Play and enter the game.
  2. Type /item festival_hat 1 and /item slime_buddy 1 in the chat.
  3. Open the bag (I) and double click each one to wear it. The portrait shows it.
Festival Hat Slime Buddy

Other players see the same: they only receive the item id at each slot and draw it with the same rules.

#If it doesn't show

Project Check lists every piece of gear that doesn't show on some player model, and why. Common causes:

  • The model has no skinned mesh named after the region, and Piece is on Auto: pick the mesh in Piece.
  • The model isn't rigged and Look is Replace: set Look to Attach.
  • The model is rigged to another skeleton: rig it to your bodies' skeleton, or use Attach.
