#CameraSettings (class)

How the camera behind the player frames the game, for the third person and free camera modes. It is part of the game's design, the same for every player. Edit it while playing to try it out: the camera follows at once and the changes stay after Play.

Member Description
public float distance Back and forth: meters behind the player when the game starts.
public Vector2 zoomRange = new Vector2(2f, 14f) How close and how far the wheel zooms. The same twice locks the zoom.
public float height Up and down: meters above the feet the camera looks at. Below zero goes under them.
public float side Left and right: meters beside the player, right above zero and left below, so the crosshair clears the body.
public CameraTurn turning Free turns all the way round. Limited stays within Yaw Range of Yaw. Fixed always faces Yaw.
public float yaw World direction the camera faces when Limited or Fixed, in degrees. 45 gives an isometric look.
public Vector2 yawRange = new Vector2(-45f, 45f) Limited: degrees it may turn left (below zero) and right of Yaw.
public float pitch Degrees the camera starts looking down.
public bool lockPitch Keeps the camera at Pitch, never tilting up or down.
public Vector2 pitchRange = new Vector2(-35f, 75f) How far it can look up (below zero) and down.
public float sensitivity Degrees per unit of mouse movement. Each player can scale it in Settings.
public float followSharpness How quickly it catches up with the player. Higher is tighter.
public bool CanTurn The mouse can move the camera at all.
public void Limit(ref float cameraYaw, ref float cameraPitch) Brings a yaw and pitch inside what this framing allows.

#CombatFormula (class)

Damage and healing. Each roll is worked out apart for its element: the roll plus the attacker's damage for that element, grown by power, then the defender's flat resistance and percent resistance (up to the element's limit). Critical hits multiply the total. Inherit and set Combat to change the rules; only the server calls it.

Member Description
public virtual float Element(StatSheet attacker, DamageElement element, float roll, StatSheet defender) One element's share of a hit, after the defender's resistances.
public virtual Hit Tick(StatSheet caster, DamageElement element, float amount, StatSheet holder) One tick of a damage over time status. Never critical; at least 1.
public virtual int Heal(StatSheet healer, float amount) A heal cast by healer: its heals stat is added, then its healing bonus.
public virtual float AttackInterval(float attacksPerSecond, StatSheet attacker) Seconds between basic attacks.
public virtual long Experience(MonsterData monster, int playerLevel) Experience for a kill, smaller when the monster is far below the player.
