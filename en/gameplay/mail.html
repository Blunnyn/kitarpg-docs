Gameplay systems

Mail

Letters between characters, online or not, with gold and up to five items attached. This page covers the mail window, postage, every limit, how long letters last, the letters the game sends by itself (market sales, shop purchases, rewards) and what the server checks.

#What it is

Every character has a mailbox kept by Central. You write to another character by name; the letter waits in their mailbox until they read it, take what is attached and delete it, or until it expires.

  • It is the Mail module (PlayerMail on the player prefab, MailWindow in the HUD, MailService in Central, table mails).
  • It is MMO only. Letters live in Central's database. In single player every mail request answers "Only on an online server."
  • Attachments are real item copies. They leave your bag when you send, belong to the letter while it travels, and become the receiver's when they take them. A copy is never in two places at once.
  • Other systems use mail to deliver things: the Market pays sellers and returns unsold lots by mail, the cash shop delivers purchases by mail, and server code can send letters from the game.

#Where it is in the editor

What Where
Turn it on or off KitARPG > Modules, module Mail. The Market and the in-game shop need it. See Modules.
Postage, items per letter, mail anywhere, shop auto take KitARPG > Game Settings, cards Mail and Shop. See Game Settings.
A mailbox NPC An Npc with a Mail option (MailOption). Only needed when Mail Anywhere is off. See NPCs.
Days, mailbox size, items per letter on the server Central's configuration, section Central. See Configuration.
The window MailWindow in the HUD prefab. The Mail button on the HUD toolbar (top right). See HUD and windows.

#Settings

#Game Settings, card Mail

Field What it does Default
Mail Anywhere (mailAnywhere) On, players read and send mail from anywhere with N. Off, only at an NPC with a Mail option, and N does nothing. On
Mail Postage (mailPostage) Gold it costs to send a letter. 10
Mail Postage Per Item (mailPostagePerItem) More gold for each item attached. 5
Mail Items (mailItems) Items one letter can carry. Central's MaxMailItems should match. 5

Postage is mailPostage + mailPostagePerItem × items. With the defaults: a letter with no items costs 10, with 3 items 25, with 5 items 35. The gold you attach is extra.

#Game Settings, card Shop

Field What it does Default
Shop Auto Take (shopAutoTake) Shop purchases and gifts go straight to the bag when they arrive. Off, or when there is no room, they wait in the mail. On

#Central (appsettings.json, section Central)

Setting What it does Default
MailDays Days an empty letter lasts. A letter with gold or items stays until they are taken. 30
MaxMailItems Items one letter may carry. A letter with more bounces back whole. 5
MaxMailbox Letters one mailbox holds. Past it, new player letters bounce. 100

With environment variables: Central__MailDays, Central__MaxMailItems, Central__MaxMailbox. See Configuration.

#HUD toolbar

Field What it does Default
Mail Badge (mailBadge) Shown on the Mail button while there are unread letters, with how many (99+ past 99). Set in the sample HUD
New Mail Text (newMailText) Floats over your character when a letter comes in. Empty says nothing. "New mail"
New Mail Color (newMailColor) Color of that text. Light gold

#MailWindow

Field What it does Default
Toggle Key (toggleKey) Opens and closes the mailbox, when Mail Anywhere is on. N
Attached Slots (attachedSlots) Slots that show a received letter's items. 5 in the sample
Attach Slots (attachSlots) Slots for items on the Write tab. You can't attach more items than there are slots. 5 in the sample

If you raise Mail Items, add slots to the window and raise MaxMailItems in Central too.

#Fixed limits

Limit Value
Recipient name 32 characters
Subject 60 characters
Text 500 characters; line breaks are kept
Gold in one letter 1,000,000,000,000,000

#Using the mailbox as a player

#Opening it

  • Press N, or click Mail on the HUD toolbar (hint "Mail (N)").
  • With Mail Anywhere off, talk to an NPC with a Mail option and pick it.

The window has two tabs: Inbox and Write. Esc closes it while you aren't typing.

#Knowing you have mail

  • When you enter the world, the chat says "You have an unread letter." or "You have 3 unread letters."
  • When a letter arrives while you play, the chat says "You have a letter from Name.", "New mail" floats over your character, and the Mail button gets a red badge with the unread count.
  • The Inbox tab reads "Inbox (3)" while there are unread letters.

Shop letters that are taken for you at once don't count as unread and don't trigger "New mail".

#The Inbox tab

On the left, the letters, newest first:

  • Unread letters have the sender's name in bold and a lighter row.
  • A gold + after the name means the letter carries gold or items.
  • The second line is the subject, or "(no subject)".

Click a letter to open it on the right. Opening an unread letter marks it read. You see:

  • The subject, then "From Name, date. Expires in N days." (or "Kept until you take what it holds." when it carries gold or items)
  • The text.
  • The gold, if any, in gold color.
  • The attached items in the slots below. Hover one for its tooltip.

Buttons:

Button What it does
Take Shown when the letter has gold or items. Takes all of it at once: gold to your purse, items to your bag.
Delete Deletes the letter. Only works once nothing is attached; otherwise the chat says "Take what's attached first."

Where taken things go:

  • Items go to the bag. If the bag is full they go to your bank: "Your bag is full, so one item went to your bank."
  • If the bag and the bank are both full, the item is held for you: "Your bag and bank are full. Free a bag slot to see the rest." Nothing is lost. See Inventory and bank.
  • If your purse would go over its limit, the rest of the gold goes to your bank: "Your purse was full, so the rest of the gold went to your bank."
  • Before asking Central, the server checks there is room in your bag or bank for the items. If not: "Make room in your bag or bank first."

#The Write tab

  1. Click Write. Your bag opens next to the mailbox.
  2. Type the recipient's character name (any case), a subject and the text.
  3. Type an amount in the gold field if you want to send gold.
  4. Attach items: right click a bag item and pick Attach to the letter, or double click it. For a stack you pick how many; hold Shift to attach one, or Ctrl / Cmd to attach the whole stack. Attaching the same stack again changes its amount.
  5. To remove an attachment, right click it in the attach slots.
  6. Check the line under the slots: "Postage 25 · Total 525 gold".
  7. Click Send. The form clears.

The gold, the postage and the items leave you right away. When Central delivers the letter you read "Letter sent to Name." If the receiver is online they read "You have a letter from YourName."

If the letter can't be delivered, everything comes back to you, postage included, with the reason:

Reason Message
No character has that name "There is no one called Name."
You wrote to yourself "You can't write to yourself."
Their mailbox has MaxMailbox letters "Name's mailbox is full."
More items than Central's MaxMailItems "Too much in one letter."
An item isn't in the catalog "That item can't be mailed."

Subjects and texts pass through the chat word filter, which always masks blocked words with asterisks for mail (it never drops a letter). Rich text tags typed by players show as plain text. See Chat.

#Letters the game sends

Some letters don't come from a player:

From Subject What it carries Notes
Market "Sold: Item" The gold of a sale. See Market.
Market "Unsold: Item" A lot nobody bought in time.
The shop The purchase's name Items from the in-game shop or a gift. Taken for you at once (see below).
Your game's code Anything Gold and items: rewards, compensation, a GM's note. Sent with MailService.SendFromGame in Central.

Letters from the game skip the mailbox size check, so they always arrive. Shop letters are written by the Shop service and expire after its own MailDays setting, not Central's. See Shop service. Items in a letter from SendFromGame get fresh ids. An item the catalog doesn't know is left out and Central logs an error.

#Shop letters are taken for you

With Shop Auto Take on, the map server takes every shop letter (Kind = shop) as soon as it sees it:

  • If there is room in your bag or bank, the items go in and the chat says "Gem Pack arrived in your bag." The letter is deleted once taken: it was only the way the purchase traveled.
  • If there is no room, you read "Gem Pack is waiting in your mail. Make room in your bag or bank and take it from there." and it counts as a new letter. Take it by hand later.

With Shop Auto Take off, shop letters stay in the mailbox like any other. See Cash shop.

#How long letters last

  • An empty letter expires MailDays (30) days after it was sent. The window shows "Expires in N days."
  • A letter with gold or items never expires: it stays until they are taken, and the window shows "Kept until you take what it holds." This covers market sales and unsold lots too.
  • Expired letters are removed when the mailbox is next listed, which happens when the owner enters a map or changes the mailbox.

#What the server checks

On the map server:

Check Message
The Mail module is on, and you are alive Ignored.
The map works for Central (MMO) "Only on an online server."
Mail Anywhere is on, or you are in reach of the NPC that opened the mailbox "Find a mailbox first."
Request pace: commands that reach Central get 2 a second, bursts of 10, per connection Ignored.
Name, subject and text within their lengths, gold not negative Ignored.
No more items than Mail Items, each bag slot only once Ignored.
You aren't writing to yourself "You can't write to yourself."
You have the gold plus postage "You need 535 gold, postage included."
No bound or quest items "Bound and quest items can't be mailed."
Taking: room in bag or bank "Make room in your bag or bank first."

In Central, sending runs under the item ledger lock and in one database transaction: the letter is written and the items move from your character to the letter together. If a copy turns out to be held by someone else, the letter is rolled back, the attempt is logged, and that copy isn't returned, because it shouldn't exist.

Taking runs one request at a time across the whole server, so an attachment is handed out exactly once. The items become yours in the same save that empties the letter. Until the map saves you, they wait as a pending delivery, so a crash loses nothing.

Sending and taking are written to the game log as mail.sent and mail.taken, with the gold and the items. See Game log and reports.

#Single player and MMO

Single player MMO
Mailbox "Only on an online server." Kept by Central for each character.
Shop letters No in-game shop in single player. Taken for you by default.
Market letters No market. Gold for sales, unsold lots.

#Step by step

#Mail only at mailboxes

  1. Open KitARPG > Game Settings, card Mail, and turn Mail Anywhere off.
  2. In each town scene, select an NPC (or a mailbox object with an Npc component) and add a Mail option.
  3. Save the scenes and test in MMO. N no longer opens the mailbox; talking to the mailbox does.

The HUD Mail button still opens the window anywhere, but every request answers "Find a mailbox first." until you are at a mailbox. Hide the button (mailButton on HudToolbar) if that confuses your players.

#Allow more items per letter

  1. Set Mail Items in Game Settings, for example to 8.
  2. Set Central:MaxMailItems to the same number and restart Central.
  3. In the HUD prefab, select the mail window and add slots to Attach Slots and Attached Slots so 8 fit.

#Make mail a gold sink

Raise Mail Postage and Mail Postage Per Item. Postage leaves the game.

#Common mistakes

  • "Too much in one letter." Mail Items in Game Settings is higher than MaxMailItems in Central.
  • A player can't delete a letter. It still has gold or items. Take them first.
  • "Only on an online server." You are testing in single player.
  • The N key does nothing. Mail Anywhere is off, or the Mail module is off, or the cursor is in a text field.
  • A letter to a friend bounces with "mailbox is full". They have MaxMailbox (100) letters. They need to delete some.
