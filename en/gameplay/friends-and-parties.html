Gameplay systems

Friends and parties

Friend lists that show who is online and where, and parties that travel together, share experience and quest kills, and enter the same dungeon copy. This page covers the windows, commands, limits, how experience is split and what changes between single player and MMO.

#What they are

  • Friends are a list of characters you know. You see whether each one is online, on which map, their level and class, and you can whisper them or invite them to your party from the list. A friendship needs both sides to agree. Friends are saved in Central's database (table friends), so they last.
  • Parties are small groups of players, up to 5 by default. Members share experience from kills near each other, quest kills count for all of them, they have their own chat channel, they enter the same copy of an instanced dungeon, and the map can stop them from hurting each other in PvP. Parties live in Central's memory: they follow members from map to map, but end when Central restarts.

Both are separate modules: Friends (PlayerFriends, FriendsWindow) and Parties (PlayerParty, PartyFrames). Both are MMO only: Central keeps them. In single player every request answers "Only on an online server."

#Where they are in the editor

What Where
Turn them on or off KitARPG > Modules, modules Friends and Parties. See Modules.
Experience share range and bonus The PlayerParty component on each player prefab.
Friend list size, party size Central's configuration, section Central. See Configuration.
The friends window FriendsWindow in the HUD prefab, and the Friends button on the HUD toolbar.
The party frames and the invite panel PartyFrames in the HUD prefab. See HUD and windows.
The player menu lines PlayerMenu in the HUD prefab.

#Settings

#Central (appsettings.json, section Central)

Setting What it does Default

MaxPartySize Members in one party, the leader included. 5

With environment variables: Central__MaxFriends, Central__MaxPartySize. Restart Central after changing them.

Field What it does Default

Bonus Per Member (bonusPerMember) Extra experience for each member past the first, as a share: 0.1 is 10%. 0.1

#FriendsWindow (HUD)

#Friends

#The friends window

Press O, or click Friends on the HUD toolbar (hint "Friends (O)"). Esc closes it.

At the top there is a name field and an Add button. Below, one row per friend or request, online friends first, then by name. Each row shows the name, level and class, and a status:

Status Buttons
Online · map name (green) Whisper, Invite (to your party), Remove
Offline (grey) Remove
Wants to be your friend (gold): they asked you Accept, Decline
Request sent (grey): you asked them Cancel

If the list is empty, the window shows its empty label.

#Adding a friend

Any of these sends a request by character name (any case):

  • Type the name in the friends window and press Add or Enter.
  • Type /friend Name in the chat.
  • Right click the player in the world (top-down) or their name in the chat, then Add friend. The line is hidden for players already on your list.

What happens:

  1. You read "Friend request sent to Name." The row shows Request sent.
  2. If they are online, they read "YourName wants to be your friend." Their row shows Wants to be your friend.
  3. They click Accept (or send you a request back, which counts as yes). You read "Name accepted your friend request."
  4. Both rows now show the friend with their online status.

Messages you may see instead:

Situation Message
No character has that name, or it is you "There is no one called Name."
Already friends "Name is already your friend."
You already asked "You already asked Name."
Your list has MaxFriends entries "Your friend list is full."

Requests work even when the other character is offline: they find it on their list when they log in.

#Removing a friend

Click Remove on the row, or type /unfriend Name. The friendship goes from both lists. Decline and Cancel remove a request the same way.

#Online notices

  • When a friend logs in, you read "Name is online." This happens once per login, not on every map change.
  • When a friend logs out, you read "Name went offline."
  • The list refreshes by itself whenever something changes: a friend logs in or out, changes map, or a request is answered.

#Parties

#Inviting

Any of these invites a player by name:

  • /invite Name in the chat.
  • Right click the player, or their name in the chat, then Invite to party. The line shows when you have no party or lead one, and the player isn't in it.
  • Invite on an online friend's row in the friends window.

The player can be on any map. The server checks, in this order:

Check Message
The player is online "Name is not online."
The player isn't in a party "Name is already in a party."
You aren't in a party, or you lead it "Only the leader can invite."
Your party has room "Your party is full."

If all pass you read "Invited Name to your party."

The invited player sees a panel: "Name invites you to a party." with Accept and Decline. It closes after 60 seconds. A newer invite replaces an older one.

  • Accept: if the inviter had no party, a new one forms with the inviter as leader. "That invite is gone." if it expired; "Whoever invited you went offline." if the inviter left; "That party is full." if it filled meanwhile.
  • Decline: the inviter reads "Name declined your invite."

#The party frames

When your party has 2 or more members, the party frames show on the HUD, one row per member:

  • Name and level.
  • Leader in gold before the leader's status.
  • The member's map name, or Offline.
  • A health bar, only while that member is close enough for your game to see them. Otherwise the bar hides.
  • Lead (make leader) and Kick buttons, for the leader only, on other members. Lead shows only for online members.
  • A Leave button for everyone.

Party members also show on the minimap and the world map. See HUD and windows.

#Managing the party

Action How Who
Leave Leave on the frames, or /leave Anyone
Remove a member Kick on their row, /kick Name, or Remove from party in the player menu Leader
Pass the lead Lead on their row, /promote Name (or /lead Name), or Make leader in the player menu Leader
Talk /p text, or make Party your default channel Anyone

When you leave or are removed you read "You are no longer in a party."

#When members go offline or leave

  • A member who logs out stays in the party and shows Offline. When they log back in they are still in it.
  • If the leader logs out or leaves, the lead passes to the first member still online.
  • A party with fewer than 2 members ends.
  • A party with nobody online ends.
  • Parties are kept in Central's memory. If Central restarts, every party ends.

#Sharing experience and quest kills

When a party member kills a monster, the members who share it are:

  • On the same map, in the same scene (the same dungeon copy).
  • Alive.
  • Within Share Range (40 m) of the monster. The killer always counts.

Each of them gets the experience the monster gives at their own level, times a share:

share = (1 + bonusPerMember × (members − 1)) / members
Members sharing Share each Total for the party
1 100% 100%
2 55% 110%
3 40% 120%
4 32.5% 130%
5 28% 140%

Then each member's own experience bonus (the Experience Gain stat and the server rates) applies, and a tax collector nearby takes its cut. See Combat for how monster experience depends on level, and Tax collectors.

Quest kill objectives count for every member who shares the kill under the same rules (same scene, alive, within Share Range). See Quests.

Loot is not shared. It goes to the player who gets the kill, the way the game's loot mode says. See Loot.

#Other things a party changes

  • Dungeons: members enter the same copy of an instanced map, even if they walk in separately. See Dungeons.
  • PvP: with the map's Pvp Spare Party on, members can't hurt each other. See PvP and duels.
  • Tax collector fights: party members near a collector join the fight with you. See Tax collectors.
  • Nameplates: the party id is visible to everyone, so the HUD can tell party members apart.

#Single player and MMO

Single player MMO
Friends "Only on an online server." Saved in Central's database.
Parties "Only on an online server." Kept in Central's memory, across maps.
Experience The killer gets it all. Shared among members near the kill.

#Step by step

#Bigger parties

  1. Set Central:MaxPartySize, for example to 8, in appsettings.json or as Central__MaxPartySize.
  2. Restart Central.
  3. Check the party frames in the HUD prefab have room for 8 rows.

#Reward grouping more

  1. Open each player prefab (PlayerMale, PlayerFemale, PlayerPanda in the sample).
  2. On PlayerParty, raise Bonus Per Member, for example to 0.2 (a party of 5 then shares 180%).
  3. Raise Share Range if your maps are large and members spread out.

PlayerParty lives on each player prefab: change all of them the same way.

#Change the split rule in code

The split is Rewards.ShareExperience on the map server, a virtual method. Override it to give the killer a larger share, add level gap rules or anything else. See Overriding kit behaviour.

#Common mistakes

  • "Only on an online server." You are testing in single player. Friends and parties need Central; switch to MMO in Play and Servers.
  • A member got no experience. They were dead, on another map or dungeon copy, or more than 40 m from the monster.
  • The party vanished after a server update. Restarting Central ends every party.
  • The invite panel stays up but Accept says "That invite is gone." Invite Seconds on PartyFrames is longer than Central's 60 seconds.
  • Different share values per character. PlayerParty is on each player prefab, and each one can have its own values.
