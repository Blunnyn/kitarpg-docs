Gameplay systems

HUD and windows

The HUD is everything on screen while playing: the bars, the quick bar, the chat, the icon bar, the minimap and every game window. It is one prefab you can restyle, rearrange and extend. This page covers what the player sees and the keys, how the HUD prefab is organized, its inspector, and how to restyle it or move a window.

#For the player

The layout keeps the middle of the screen free and puts everything on the edges.

Where What
Bottom left Chat (500 × 270), with a "CHAT" strip
Bottom, centered in the space the chat leaves Bottom bar: health, mana, level, experience and the 10 quick bar slots, with buffs
Top right Icon bar: one button per window, plus the shop, the world map, the bestiary and the game menu
Left of the icon bar Clock: day or night and the hour
Under the icon bar Minimap, then the quest tracker

The boss bar appears when a boss or elite is close, and the party frames list your party members with their health.

#The icon bar

Hovering a button shows the window's name and key. Unspent attribute or skill points show as a small badge on their button, and unread mail as a count on the Mail button. When a letter arrives, "New mail" floats over the player. Buttons of modules your game turned off don't appear, and the shop button only shows in online games.

#Keys

Key Window
I Bag
C Character
K Skills
L Quest log
M World map
B Bestiary
O Friends
G Guild
N Mail
P Cash shop
F8 GM panel (moderators and up)
Enter Write in the chat
Esc Close the open window, or open the game menu

Each window's key is a field on its script in the HUD prefab (Toggle Key), so you can change it. The full list of keys is in Keys.

#Chat

Press Enter or click anywhere in the chat box to type. Enter sends and lets go of the keyboard, Esc cancels. The box darkens while you type. See Chat.

#Escape

Esc first closes whatever window or popup is open. Only when nothing was open does it open the game menu. While you type in the chat, answer a confirmation or have Settings open, Esc doesn't open the menu.

#Minimap

The round minimap shows the map name on top and the area around you. A camera looks down from above and turns with the game camera, so up on the minimap is up on screen. Portals, party members and bosses stay on the edge when they are far. Zoom with the + and − buttons or the mouse wheel. Click it to open the world map. At night it looks dark, like the scene.

#World map

M opens a view from above of the whole map, with the names of NPCs, bosses and party members, and where each portal leads. Clicking a point sends your character walking there; the server finds the path. It also works inside dungeon copies.

The marks are NPCs, portals, monsters (normal, elite and boss), other players and your party, refreshed every 0,4 seconds.

#Item and player menus

  • Right click an item in the bag or the equipment for the item menu.
  • Right click another player (in top-down), or their name in chat, for the player menu: Whisper, Trade, Invite to party, Add friend, Invite to guild, Duel and Report.

#The HUD prefab

The HUD is the prefab in Game Settings > Interface > Hud Prefab (hudPrefab). The sample's is Samples/Starter/Prefabs/GameHud.prefab. It is made once on each client when the game starts and kept between maps. A server without graphics doesn't make it.

Field What it does Default
Hud Prefab (hudPrefab) Canvas with the HUD and the game windows. Empty: the scenes bring their own Sample: GameHud
Item Details On Hover (itemDetailsOnHover) Pointing at an item shows its stats. Off: click the item to see them Off

The canvas scales with the screen from a reference of 1920 × 1080. Players scale it further with Interface scale in Settings.

#How it is organized

The root of the prefab has four groups, drawn in this order (later ones on top):

Group What it holds
HUD Bottom bar, boss bar, quest tracker, icon bar, clock, chat, party frames, minimap
Windows Bag, character, skills, loot, NPC dialogs, quests, friends, guild and its alliance, emblem and history windows, trade, bank, forge, NPC shop, mail, sets, market, bestiary, world map, cash shop, GM panel, settings
Popups Player menu, item menu and the confirmation dialog
Overlay The item tooltip and the dragged item icon, shared by many windows

Inside each group, every script has its own object, stretched to the whole screen and with no image, holding the panels it shows and hides. The panel is a child of that object, so the script keeps running while its panel is closed (that is how window keys work).

The set window and the confirmation dialog move their own object to the top when they open, so they cover other windows.

#Rows are prefabs

Windows that show lists (guild members, mail, bank slots, quest rows, shop cards) copy a row for each entry. Those rows are prefabs in Samples/Starter/Prefabs/UI, such as Guild Row, Mail Row, Bank Slot, Cash Shop Offer or Bestiary Row. Edit the prefab once and every row changes. Rows that are instances of another prefab, like the item slot (UIItem), are prefab variants.

Each window's fields ending in Template point at these rows. A row template can also sit inside the window, hidden; the window then puts copies next to it.

#The inspector of a HUD piece

Select any HUD script's object and its inspector shows:

  • What the piece does, and which of its parts are still empty ("All parts set" or "3 empty"). Some parts are optional; the tooltips say which.
  • Select panel and Show panel / Hide panel, to see a closed window while you edit it.
  • Rows it copies: each row prefab with Open, or Select when the row lives in the window.
  • The fields in cards, by section.

#Step by step: restyle the HUD

  1. Work on your own copy. Duplicate GameHud.prefab into your game's folder (Ctrl/Cmd+D, then move it). Set Hud Prefab in Game Settings > Interface to the copy. Rebuilding the sample starts it from scratch, so never edit the sample's prefab for your game.
  2. Open the prefab (double click). Expand Windows and select the window you want, for example Inventory Window.
  3. Press Show panel in its inspector to see it.
  4. Change images, colors, fonts (TextMeshPro) and sizes on its panel's children like any uGUI. Keep the names of children a window looks up by name (the tooltips list them, such as Name, Status, Label, Art, Badge).
  5. For list rows, press Open next to the row under Rows it copies and edit the row prefab.
  6. Press Hide panel, save the prefab and press Play.
Tip

Colors that change with state, such as tab colors, row highlight, the chat's typing color or badge colors, are fields on the window's script, not on the images. Look in the script's cards.

#Step by step: move a window

  1. Open your HUD prefab and select the window's panel (its object's inspector has Select panel).
  2. In the Rect Transform, change the anchors and position. For example, to put the bag on the left: anchor preset middle left, pivot (0, 0.5), position X 20.
  3. Keep the panel under its script's object, so the script keeps running while the panel is closed and kit updates find it.
  4. Save and press Play.

To move a whole HUD piece, such as the minimap or the icon bar, move its panel the same way inside the HUD group.

#Step by step: change a window key

  1. Select the window's script object in the HUD prefab, for example Bestiary Window.
  2. Change Toggle Key.
  3. Update the hint on its icon bar button if you show the key there.

#Removing a piece

Turn off a module in Modules instead of deleting its window: buttons and keys of modules that are off go away by themselves. To drop a piece the game doesn't need, such as the clock, delete its object from the HUD group. Most links between pieces are optional, and the inspector shows what each piece still has empty.

#Kit updates and your HUD

When a newer kit version adds windows or buttons, it does so with kit updates that work on the prefab in Hud Prefab, adding what is missing without rebuilding the rest. Examples: Guild roles, emblem, history and alliance windows in the HUD, Shop window in the HUD, New shop window: categories, banner and offer pages. Overview lists the ones your project needs with Apply. See Updating the kit.

#Touch screens

On phones and tablets the touch controls sit on their own canvas next to the HUD, and fit the HUD around them. See Touch controls.

#In code

Every window is a MonoBehaviour with virtual methods, so you can subclass one and swap it in the prefab. MapPoints.Extra adds marks of your own to the minimap and world map. The item and player menus take extra lines through ItemMenu.Entries and PlayerMenu.Entries. See Overriding kit behaviour.

#Single player and MMO

The HUD is the same in both. Windows of online-only systems (guild, mail, market, cash shop) show messages such as "Only on an online server." or don't open in single player.

#Common mistakes

  • My changes to the HUD are gone. You edited the sample's GameHud.prefab and rebuilt the sample. Work on a copy.
  • A window doesn't open with its key. Its module is off, its Toggle Key changed, or its script's object was disabled. Disable the panel, never the object holding the script.
  • Rows show empty. A child the window looks up by name was renamed. Check the Template field's tooltip for the names.
