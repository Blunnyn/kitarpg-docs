Gameplay systems

Inventory and bank

How the bag, the worn gear, money, loot pickup and the account bank work for the player, every setting that changes them, the limits the server enforces and how they differ between single player and MMO.

#What it is

Every character has a bag, a set of equipment positions and a purse of gold and other currencies. The account also has a bank: slots and gold that every character of the account shares, opened at a banker NPC.

The server owns all of it. The client only sends requests (move, wear, use, split, store) and the server answers with the new slots or an error code. Only the owner receives the contents of the bag and bank; other players see only the worn gear, through the character's appearance.

The rules live in one shared class, Inventory (Runtime/Shared/Items/Inventory.cs), used by the map server, the single player host and the services alike. On the player prefab the network side is PlayerInventory (bag, gear, money) and PlayerBank (bank).

#Settings

All bag, loot and bank settings are on the GameInstance component of the KitNetwork prefab. Edit them in KitARPG > Game Settings (or the Game Settings page of the KitARPG window, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+K).

#Bag

Field What it does Default
Bag Mode (bagMode) Slots: a fixed number of slots. Weight: anything fits up to a weight limit. Slots
Bag Slots (bagSlots) Slots in a bag in Slots mode. 40
Weight Mode Slots (weightModeSlots) In Weight mode, how many different stacks a bag can hold, so it can't grow forever. 200
Carry Weight (carryWeight) In Weight mode, what anyone can carry. Gear and attributes add their carry weight stat on top. 1,000
Bank Slots (bankSlots) Slots in the account bank. The bank has no weight limit. 80

#Loot

Field What it does Default
Loot Mode (lootMode) Ground: each item falls next to the monster. Corpse: items stay in the body and clicking it opens a loot window. Automatic: items go straight to the bag, and what doesn't fit falls on the ground. Ground
Auto Gold (autoGold) Gold goes straight to the player. Off: it drops like the items, on the ground or in the body. On

#Interface and economy

Field What it does Default
Item Details On Hover (itemDetailsOnHover) On: pointing at an item shows its card. Off: click an item to pin its card beside it. Off
Players Sell To Npcs (playersSellToNpcs) Shops buy what players bring. Off, nothing is sold to NPCs. See NPC shops. Off

#Modules

The bank is the Bank module. Turn it off in KitARPG > Modules and the banker option disappears from NPCs and the server refuses to open the bank. The bag itself is not a module; it is always there.

#On the player prefab

Component and field What it does Default
PlayerInventory.maxRequestsPerSecond Bag requests per second before the extra ones are ignored. 15

#On the map server

The Rewards component (on the map server object) decides how long loot stays and how close the player must be.

Field What it does Default
dropLifetime Seconds a drop stays before it disappears. 90
pickupRange Meters from the drop to pick it up. 2
scatter Ground drops land within this many meters of the monster. 1.2

#The inventory window

Press I to open or close the inventory. Esc first puts away a pinned item card, then closes the window. The HUD has a bag button that does the same.

The window has two parts.

The character. A live portrait of the character in its gear, with its name, level and class, surrounded by the equipment positions:

Position Holds
Head, Chest, Hands, Legs, Feet, Back Armour pieces for each body region.
Main hand, Off hand Weapons, shields and off-hand items.
Ring, Second ring Two rings. A ring goes to the second position when the first is taken.
Amulet One amulet.
Ammo A stack of arrows or bolts for weapons that use ammunition.

The Show cosmetics button switches these slots to the eight cosmetic positions (Head, Chest, Hands, Legs, Feet, Back, Main hand, Off hand). Cosmetics change how the region looks and give no stats. The button then reads Show gear. Opening the Cosmetics tab of the bag switches to the cosmetic positions on its own. See Equipment.

The bag. A grid of slots with:

  • Tabs: All, Equipment, Consumables, Resources and Cosmetics. Resources are items that are neither gear nor usable.
  • Search: filters by name as you type.
  • Sort list: By slot, By name, By type, By rarity, By weight. This only changes the view; the items keep their slots.
  • Type list: narrows the tab to one item category. It is filled from what is in the bag.
  • Sort button: asks the server to tidy the bag. It joins stacks and packs everything from the first slot: gear first (by equipment position), then the rest by item id.
  • Capacity bar: used slots out of the bag size, or weight out of the limit in Weight mode. It turns red at 90% full. When the character is overloaded the label reads "Too heavy to move" in red.
  • Money: gold, and below it every other currency marked to show in the inventory. See Currencies.

With the All tab, no search, no type and "By slot", the grid shows every slot in order. With any filter, or in Weight mode, the grid shows only the matching items packed together, followed by some empty slots to drop onto.

#Mouse actions

Action What it does
Drag an item onto another slot Moves it. Onto the same item it tops up the stack; onto a different item it swaps them.
Drag onto an equipment position Wears it. Whatever was there takes its place in the bag.
Double click a bag item Wears gear, uses a consumable. With a bank, trade, market or forge open beside the bag, does what that window does instead (store, offer, sell, put in the forge).
Double click worn gear Takes it off into the first free bag slot.
Shift + double click a stack Splits off half into the first free slot.
Right click Opens the item menu.
Click (details on hover off) Pins the item card beside the item. Click again, or an empty slot, to put it away.

#The item menu

Right click an item in the bag or the gear to open ItemMenu. It lists only the lines that apply:

Line Shows when
The open window's action (Store, Offer, Put in the forge...) A bank, trade, market or forge window is open beside the bag.
Equip The bag item is gear.
Take off The item is worn.
Use The bag item is usable and not gear.
Split The stack has more than one. Asks how many, half to start with, up to one less than the stack.
Salvage all (N) The forge is open and the bag has more than one plain copy of this piece. See Forge.
Possible values The piece has random bonuses: pins its card with the range each bonus could have had.
View set The piece belongs to a set: opens the set window. See Sets, upgrades and sockets.
Destroy The item is in the bag. Asks to confirm, then deletes it for good. Worn gear has to come off first.

Games add their own lines to ItemMenu.Entries.

#Messages

When the server refuses a request, the window shows a short message for 3 seconds:

Code Message
bag_full Your bag is full.
too_heavy You can't carry any more.
wrong_slot That doesn't go there.
level_too_low Your level is too low.
wrong_class Your class can't use that.
not_usable You can't use that now.
cooldown Not ready yet.
unknown_item That item no longer exists in the game.

Override InventoryWindow.Describe to translate them.

#Bag rules

These rules are the same in single player and MMO, because both run the same Inventory class on the server.

#Stacks

  • Each item has a Max Stack (maxStack): 99 for plain items by default, 1 for gear.
  • Adding an item tops up existing stacks first, then fills free slots.
  • Bound and unbound copies of the same item never share a stack.
  • A copy with random bonuses (rolls) never stacks: each one is its own, with its own values. See Items.
  • Taking items for a cost (a quest, an upgrade) takes from the smallest stacks first.

#Wearing gear

  • The server checks the level and the class the piece asks for before it goes on.
  • A two-handed weapon and an off-hand item can't be worn together. The one already worn goes to the bag; if the bag has no free slot, nothing changes and you see "Your bag is full."
  • A one-handed weapon whose type allows dual wielding goes to the free off hand when the main hand already holds one that allows it too.
  • Taking gear off onto a bag item that can't go where the gear was puts the gear in any free slot instead.
  • Every move either completes or leaves everything as it was.

#Using items

Double click or Use a consumable. Items in the same cooldown group share one cooldown. Consumables lose one per use. Drag an item onto the hotbar to use it with a number key; the key uses the first stack of that item in the bag.

#Bag in Weight mode

  • The limit is carryWeight plus the character's carry weight stat. In the sample, each point of Strength adds 5, and backpacks add 10 or 20.
  • The weight counts everything the character has, worn gear included.
  • Each item has a Weight (weight), 1 by default. Items with weight 0 never count.
  • weightModeSlots still limits how many different stacks fit.

#Overloaded

Loot is the one thing that may go past the weight limit. When picked-up loot puts the character over it, the character is overloaded:

  • It can't walk. Click-to-move is refused and the current path stops, in top-down and in third person.
  • The server says "Too heavy to move. Drop, sell or store something." The bag shows the weight in red.
  • Drop, destroy, store or sell something to walk again.

Everything else that adds items (buying, trading, taking from the bank or the mail) refuses when it would go over the limit.

#When the bag gets smaller

If you lower bagSlots, or two saved items end up in the same slot, the bag moves the extra items to free slots when it loads. Nothing is deleted.

#Money

  • Gold is the purse currency. Its Max Amount (maxAmount) on the gold CurrencyData caps the purse; 0 means no cap.
  • Gold the purse can't hold (from loot, mail, the guild or quests) goes to the bank gold instead of being lost. The server says "(some to the bank)".
  • Other currencies show under the gold when their showInInventory is on.
  • Premium currency is never in the purse. It belongs to the account and is handled by the shop service. See Cash shop.

#Picking up loot

Loot belongs only to the player who earned the kill. Nobody else sees it or can take it. It lives on the server for dropLifetime seconds (90) and then disappears.

Loot mode What the player sees
Ground Each item floats on the ground with its icon and its name in its rarity colour, plus a pile of gold when autoGold is off. Click it to walk there and pick it up.
Corpse The monster's grey body stays. Click it to walk there; the loot window opens with one row per item and a gold row. Click a row to take it, or Take all. The window closes when the body is empty, when you walk away or with Esc.
Automatic Items go straight to the bag with a "+Item" message. What doesn't fit falls on the ground.

You must be within pickupRange (2 m) of the drop, with 1.5 m of extra room while the loot window is open. When the bag has no free slot, what doesn't fit stays on the ground or in the body and you see the reason.

#Items from elsewhere never get lost

Items that come from somewhere other than loot or a shop (a letter, the guild bank, a tax collector, a quest reward) follow this order:

  1. The bag.
  2. The bank, if the bag is full or too heavy.
  3. Held outside the bag grid, if the bank is full too.

A held item counts, is saved and takes the first free slot the next time the bag loads (on the next map change or login). Before taking a letter or withdrawing from the guild bank, the map server checks that everything fits in the bag or the bank, and refuses with a message if it doesn't.

#The bank

The bank is one per account: every character of the account sees the same slots and the same bank gold. It has bankSlots slots (80) and no weight limit.

#Opening it

Talk to an NPC with a Bank option (BankOption, see NPCs). In the sample it is Odette, the banker in Town. The bank window opens with the bag beside it.

The bank only works while you stand next to the banker: within the NPC's Talk Range (talkRange, 3 m by default) plus half a meter. Walk away, die or leave the map and the window closes.

#The bank window

  • The title reads BANK.
  • A grid with every bank slot.
  • A Sort button that joins stacks and packs the bank, like the bag's.
  • The gold area: the bank gold, a number field and Deposit and Withdraw buttons.

#Storing and taking out

Action What it does
Double click a bag item, or Store in its menu Stores it.
Drag a bag item onto the bank Stores it.
Double click or right click a bank item Takes it out to the bag.
Drag inside the bank Moves, stacks or swaps bank items.

For stacks, the amount follows the keys held:

  • Shift: one.
  • Ctrl or Cmd: the whole stack.
  • No key: a dialog asks how many, starting at the whole stack.

For gold, type an amount and press Deposit or Withdraw. Withdrawing stops at what the purse can still hold, so no gold vanishes.

#What the server checks

  • You are at an open bank, alive and in reach of the banker.
  • Quest items can't be stored: "Quest items stay with you." They follow the character's quests.
  • Bound items can be stored, because they stay in the same account.
  • A full bank refuses with "The bank is full."
  • Taking out needs room in the bag (slots and weight): "Make room in your bag first."
  • Every deposit and withdrawal goes to the game log. See Game log and reports.

The bank is never smaller than what it holds. If you lower bankSlots below what an account already stores, that account's bank keeps enough slots.

#The guild bank

The guild bank uses the same window, opened from an NPC with a Guild bank option. Its title is the guild's name, it has no Sort button, and depositing and withdrawing gold depend on the member's role. Its size is set in Central (GuildStorageSlots, 50). See Guilds.

#Single player and MMO

Single player MMO
Rules Same Inventory rules, run by the host. Same rules, run by the map server.
Bag and gear saved in The character's JSON in the local SQLite save. Rows of item_instances owned by the character, through Central.
Bank saved in An account table in the local save, shared by all characters. Rows of item_instances owned by the account, and accounts.BankGold.
Copies Each copy has a unique id (uid). Each copy is one row with exactly one owner. Central's ItemLedger moves rows between owners in transactions and refuses a uid another owner holds.

Because there is one session per account, the character in play carries the account bank and saves it with itself. In MMO this means the bank can't be open on two characters at once.

#Step by step

#Make the bag limited by weight

  1. Open KitARPG > Game Settings, section Bag.
  2. Set Bag Mode to Weight.
  3. Set Carry Weight to the base everyone carries, for example 1,000.
  4. Give items a Weight in the Game Database. Gear and materials that should be free get 0.
  5. Add the carry weight stat to attributes or gear (for example per point of Strength, or on backpacks).
  6. Press Play and open the bag: the bar now shows weight out of the limit.

#Add a banker

  1. Place an NPC in a map scene (see NPCs).
  2. In its options add Bank Option. The label defaults to "Bank".
  3. Make sure the Bank module is on in KitARPG > Modules.
  4. Press Play, talk to the NPC and choose Bank.

#Switch to loot in the body

  1. In KitARPG > Game Settings, section Loot, set Loot Mode to Corpse.
  2. Turn Auto Gold off if gold should wait in the body too.
  3. Kill a monster and click its body to open the loot window.

#Tips

  • Turn Item Details On Hover on for a Diablo-like feel; leave it off for a Dofus-like detail card you pin with a click.
  • The view sort (By rarity, By weight) is free and instant. The Sort button changes the real slots on the server.
  • Players who carry a lot should store materials in the bank: it has no weight limit.

#Common mistakes

  • "Your bag is full" when equipping a two-handed weapon. The off-hand item has to go to the bag and there is no free slot.
  • Character won't move after looting. It is overloaded in Weight mode. Store or drop something.
  • Quest items won't go in the bank. That is by design.
  • The bank closes by itself. You walked out of the banker's reach.
  • Lowering Bag Slots to shrink existing bags. Existing items are kept and moved to free slots; if there aren't enough slots they wait outside the grid.
