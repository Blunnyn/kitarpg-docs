Gameplay systems

Tax collectors

A tax collector is a guild's agent that stands at a spot on a map and keeps a share of the gold and experience of every kill around it, and finds items of its own. Other players can attack it, and its guild is warned and has time to come and defend it. This page covers how collectors work for players, every setting, what the server checks, and how to add collector spots to your maps.

#How it works

  1. A guild member whose role may place collectors walks to a collector spot (a signpost, a stall, any NPC with the spot component) and picks Place a tax collector. The guild pays gold.
  2. The collector appears next to the spot. From then on, every monster killed within the spot's radius pays it a share of its gold and of the killer's experience, and rolls the monster's loot once more for the collector.
  3. A member whose role may collect comes back and picks Collect. They get the gold and items, the guild gets the experience, and the collector goes home.
  4. Meanwhile, players of other guilds may attack it. If they bring it down, the one who did the most damage takes everything it gathered.

Collectors are inspired by the tax collectors (percepteurs) of Dofus.

#For the player

#At the spot

The spot's NPC shows only the options that apply:

Option Shows when Label
Place a tax collector No collector stands at the spot and you are in a guild "Place a tax collector (cost gold)" with the current price
Collect Your guild's collector stands there "Collect: gold, items, XP" with what it gathered
Attack the tax collector Another guild's collector stands there and its fight hasn't started "Attack the tax collector of guild", or "Join the attack on guild's collector" while it prepares

#The sign over it

The nameplate over the collector shows its guild and whether it is under attack or fighting.

#The guild window

The Collectors tab of the guild window (G) shows:

  • Your tax collectors: the guild's current numbers, such as "2 collectors out at once", "Holds 70 items", "25% to find each item a kill drops", "Takes 6% of the experience", "Takes 6% of the gold".
  • Each upgrade, its current rank and maximum, what it adds and the guild level the next rank needs, with Raise for roles allowed to spend points.
  • Each collector out: the map, the spot, who placed it, and the gold, experience and item count at its last save. "None placed" when there are none.

#Being attacked

When someone attacks your collector, every online member of your guild reads, on any map: "Your tax collector in map is under attack by name! The fight starts in 30 seconds." When the fight starts they read that too, and at the end whether it held off its attackers or fell.

#What it gathers

On each kill within the spot's radius, the collector:

  • Takes its Tax percent of the gold the monster drops.
  • Takes its Wisdom percent of the experience the killer earns, for the guild.
  • Rolls, for each drop of the monster's loot table, its Prospecting percent chance to get one of its own, until it holds its Capacity of items. Quest items never.

Small shares round at random, so weak monsters still pay on average. By default a collector doesn't tax its own guild's kills (see taxesItsGuild).

Collected items get their random bonuses when they arrive in the member's bag. If the bag is full they go to the account bank (see Inventory and bank).

#The fight

Phase What happens
Peace It gathers. It doesn't fight anyone.
Preparing A player of the required level called the attack. They and their party members close by are the attackers. Others may join the attack during this time. Its guild is warned and has Prepare Seconds (30) to come.
Fighting Only the attackers can damage the collector, and it only hits them. Near it, the attackers and the guild's members can fight each other on any map, whatever the map's PvP rules. The attackers have Fight Seconds (180) to bring it down.
  • It falls: the attacker who did the most damage takes everything it gathered, and it is gone for good.
  • It holds: if every attacker dies, leaves or walks out of the radius, or time runs out, it heals to full and goes back to peace. Its guild is told.

A collector can't be collected while it is under attack ("It can't leave while it is under attack.").

#Rules the server checks

When placing:

  • Collectors and guilds are on in the game, and the server is online.
  • The player is in a guild whose role has the Place collectors permission.
  • No collector stands at the spot, or one was placed there a moment ago.
  • One collector per guild on each map ("Your guild already has a collector on this map.").
  • The guild has fewer collectors out than its Collectors number.
  • The player has the gold. The map takes it first, and Central gives it back if it refuses (no permission, spot taken, limit reached).

When collecting: the collector belongs to the player's guild, their role has Collect, and it is at peace.

When attacking: the collector belongs to another guild and the player is at least Attack Level.

#Setting it up

#The Tax Collector asset

The game has one tax collector, set in the Game Database Settings, field Collector (collector). Empty turns collectors off. Create one with Create > KitARPG > Tax Collector.

Field What it does Default
Monster (monster) What stands and fights. Its prefab needs a TaxCollector component besides the Monster Empty
Place Cost (placeCost) Gold to place one 500
Cost Per Placed (costPerPlaced) More gold for each collector the guild already has out 250
Collectors (collectors) How many the guild may have out at once, before upgrades 1
Capacity (capacity) Items it holds before it stops picking them up 50
Prospecting (prospecting) Percent chance, per drop of a kill, to get one of its own 20
Wisdom (wisdom) Percent of the killer's experience it takes for the guild 5
Tax (tax) Percent of the gold of a kill it takes 5
Taxes Its Guild (taxesItsGuild) Also takes from its own guild's kills Off
Upgrades (upgrades) What the guild buys with its points Empty
Health Per Guild Level (healthPerGuildLevel) Health on top of the monster's, per guild level past the first 60
Attack Level (attackLevel) Level a player needs to attack one 5
Prepare Seconds (prepareSeconds) Between the attack being called and the fight 30
Fight Seconds (fightSeconds) Time the attackers have to bring it down 180
Save Seconds (saveSeconds) Between saves of what it gathered 30

#Upgrades

Each upgrade raises one number a rank at a time, paid with guild points (one point per rank).

Field What it does Default
Id (id) Saved with the guild. Don't change it once guilds use it Empty
Name (name) Shown in the guild window Empty
Stat (stat) Collectors, Capacity, Prospecting, Wisdom or Tax Collectors
Max Rank (maxRank) Highest rank 5
Per Rank (perRank) What each rank adds 1
Guild Level (guildLevel) Guild level needed for the first rank 2
Levels Per Rank (levelsPerRank) More guild levels needed for each rank after the first 1

The sample's upgrades:

Upgrade Stat Ranks Per rank First rank at guild level Then every
Deep Pockets Capacity 5 +20 items 2 1 level
Keen Eye Prospecting 5 +5% 2 1 level
Ledger Wisdom 5 +1% 3 1 level
Toll Tax 5 +1% 3 1 level
More Collectors Collectors 3 +1 4 4 levels

#The collector monster

Make a monster for it and add a TaxCollector component to its prefab next to Monster. Turn off In Bestiary so it doesn't show in the bestiary. The sample's is a voxel human in noble clothes, level 6, 420 health, which doesn't wander.

#Step by step: add a collector spot

  1. In your map's scene, add an NPC (a signpost or a stall works well). See NPCs.
  2. Add a CollectorSpot component to it.
    Field What it does Default
    Radius (radius) Kills within this many meters pay the collector. Its fights happen inside it too 15
    Stand (stand) Where the collector stands, from the spot (1.5, 0, 0)
    The Scene view draws the radius as a yellow circle and where the collector stands as a box.
  3. On the NPC, add three Collector options (CollectorOption) with Act Place, Collect and Attack. Each shows only when it applies.
  4. Put the spot where monsters live, so kills fall inside the radius.
  5. Rebuild the map server and try it with two accounts in different guilds (see Play & Servers).

The sample's forest has two spots, one by the slimes and one by the mushrooms.

#Single player and MMO

Collectors work only in MMO. Central (CollectorService, table collectors) keeps each collector with what it gathered, so it survives map server restarts. The map sends what each collector holds every Save Seconds and Central sends each map the list of its collectors when it registers and whenever something changes. In single player the options don't show.

Turn collectors off with the Tax collectors module in Modules. It needs the Guilds module.

#Common mistakes

  • The options never show. The NPC has no CollectorSpot, the game is single player, or Collector is empty in the Game Database.
  • "Tax collectors are off in this game." The Tax collectors or Guilds module is off, or Collector is empty.
  • The collector appears but never fights. Its prefab lacks the TaxCollector component.
  • It gathers nothing. The spot's radius doesn't reach where monsters die, or only its own guild hunts there and Taxes Its Guild is off.
  • Upgrades reset. An upgrade's Id was changed after guilds bought ranks.
