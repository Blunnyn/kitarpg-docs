Gameplay systems

PvP and duels

Players can fight each other in two ways: open PvP where a map allows it (nowhere, inside PvP zones, or everywhere), and duels, which two players agree to and which work on any map. This page covers what players see, every setting per map, what the server checks, and how to make a PvP zone.

#For the player

#Hostile players

A player you may attack has their name in red over their head. In top-down, a left click on a hostile player attacks them, and skills aimed at enemies also target hostile players. In the other control modes you attack them like monsters, where you aim.

On a map with PvP zones, entering or leaving one says so in chat: "You entered a PvP zone." and "You left the PvP zone."

Killing another player gives no experience. Both players read who defeated whom.

#Duels

  1. Right click another player (or their name in chat) and pick Duel from the player menu.
  2. They get a popup to accept or decline. The challenge lasts 30 seconds.
  3. On accepting, both read "Duel with name: fight!" and can hit each other, on any map, even where PvP is off.

A duel ends when:

  • One of them takes a blow that would bring them to 0 health. They stay at 1 health and the other wins.
  • They move more than 30 meters apart (the duel range plus 10).
  • One of them leaves the map or disconnects.
  • One gives up with Give up the duel.

Duels never cost experience or anything else.

You can't challenge someone who is more than 20 meters away, already dueling, or dead. A new challenge has to wait 2 seconds after the last one.

#Map rules

Each map sets its own PvP rules in the Game Database (Maps), section Player against player.

Field What it does Default
Pvp (pvp) Off: nobody fights other players outside duels. Zones: only when both players are inside a PvP zone. Everywhere: anywhere on the map Off
Pvp Min Level (pvpMinLevel) Players below this level can't attack others, nor be attacked 1
Pvp Spare Party (pvpSpareParty) Members of the same party never hurt each other On
Pvp Spare Guild (pvpSpareGuild) Members of the same guild never hurt each other On

The sample's forest has PvP only in zones, from level 5, with a red sand arena to the east.

#What decides if one player may hurt another

The kit asks PlayerPvp.CanHurt(a, b), on the server and on the clients, in this order:

  1. Both are players on the same map (the same copy of a dungeon).
  2. If the PvP module is off, only the tax collector fight rule applies.
  3. If they are dueling each other: yes.
  4. If they are on opposite sides of a tax collector fight, near the collector: yes.
  5. The map's Pvp is Off: no.
  6. Pvp is Zones and they aren't both inside a zone: no.
  7. Either is below Pvp Min Level: no.
  8. Same party with Pvp Spare Party on, or same guild with Pvp Spare Guild on: no.
  9. Otherwise: yes.

The server applies it to every hit: damage from a player who may not hurt the target is dropped. Attacks, area skills and target picking all follow the same rule, so a fireball in a crowd only hurts those it may.

#Death in PvP

A player killed by another player comes back at the spawn point like any death (see Recovery and death). Whether they lose experience is a game setting:

Field What it does Default
Pvp Death Costs Experience (pvpDeathCostsExperience) Players killed by other players lose experience too, by Death Experience Loss Off

It is in KitARPG > Game Settings, section Death. Duels never cost anything.

Every PvP kill is written to the game log as combat.pvp_kill.

#Step by step: make a PvP zone

  1. In the Game Database, open the map and set Pvp to Zones. Set Pvp Min Level if new players should be safe.
  2. In the map's scene, make an empty GameObject where the arena is and add a PvpZone component.
    Field What it does Default
    Radius (radius) Size of the circle on the ground, in meters. Height doesn't matter 15
    The Scene view draws the circle in red.
  3. Add more PvpZone objects for more areas. A player is in a zone if they are inside any of them.
  4. Mark the place for players: a floor of another color, a fence, a sign. The zone itself is invisible in the game.
  5. Rebuild the map server for MMO (see Builds).

The server checks each player's position every half second. Both players must be inside a zone at the moment of the hit.

For a whole PvP map, set Pvp to Everywhere and skip the zones.

#Single player and MMO

PvP and duels need other players, so they matter in MMO. They also work with two players on your computer (see Play & Servers). Turn both off with the PvP and duels module in Modules: then only tax collector fights remain.

#In code

PlayerPvp.ServerDefeated fires on the server with the winner, the loser and whether it was a duel, for rankings or rewards. PlayerPvp sits on the player prefab, with Duel Range (duelRange, 20) and Request Seconds (requestSeconds, 30). See Player systems and Events.

#Common mistakes

  • Players can't hit each other in the zone. The map's Pvp is still Off, the PvpZone is in another scene than the map, or one of them is below Pvp Min Level.
  • Guild mates fight in the arena only when they leave the guild. Pvp Spare Guild is on. Turn it off for an arena where everyone fights.
  • Names aren't red. Only players you may hurt right now are red. Check that both are inside the zone.
