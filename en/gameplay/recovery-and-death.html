Gameplay systems

Recovery and death

How players get health and mana back, when they count as in combat, what happens when they die, how they come back (on their own or revived by an ally) and what it costs. Every rule here is a setting in KitARPG > Game Settings, sections Recovery and Death.

#Overview

  • Health and mana regenerate every second from the character's healthRegen and manaRegen stats.
  • A player is in combat for a few seconds after hitting or being hit. Combat can slow or stop regeneration.
  • Reaching a new level can fill health and mana.
  • A player who falls lies on the ground waiting for a revive. After a short wait they can press Respawn; after 15 seconds they come back on their own at the map's spawn point.
  • Coming back at the spawn point costs part of the level's experience. Being revived by an ally costs nothing.
  • After coming back, the player is protected for a few seconds.

All of it runs on the server. Clients only see the result and press the Respawn button, which the server checks.

#In combat

A player enters combat when they:

  • take a hit, even one fully absorbed by a shield or stopped by invulnerability;
  • deal a hit to anyone, or one of their summons does;
  • take a damage over time tick.

They stay in combat for Combat Seconds (combatSeconds, default 6) after the last of those. Healing someone doesn't put you in combat; it does earn threat with the monsters fighting them (see Combat).

Combat ends at once on coming back at the spawn point.

Other systems can read and extend it from code: PlayerCharacter.InCombat on the server, ServerEnterCombat() to put a player in combat and PlayerCharacter.ServerMarkCombat(source) to mark whoever is behind a hit, the owner of a summon included. See Player systems.

#Regeneration

Every physics step the server adds regeneration to a running total and gives whole points as they build up, so a regeneration of 0.2 per second gives 1 health every 5 seconds.

per second = regen stat × rate
rate = 1                    in combat, with Regen In Combat on
       0                    in combat, with Regen In Combat off
       Out Of Combat Regen  out of combat
  • healthRegen and manaRegen are per second. They come from the class base stats, attributes (in the sample, each Vitality point gives 0.2 health regeneration and each Intelligence point 0.08 mana regeneration), gear and buffs. See Attributes and stats.
  • Statuses with healing or mana ticks (Regeneration in the sample) restore on top of this, in or out of combat. See Statuses and buffs.
  • Dead players don't regenerate.
  • Monsters don't regenerate. A monster that gives up a chase heals to full when it turns back home.

#Level up

When experience fills the bar, the player levels up as many times as the experience covers. Each level gives attribute and skill points and recalculates stats. "Level N!" appears over the character in gold. With Level Up Restores Health and Level Up Restores Mana on (the default), health and mana fill up.

#Death

When health reaches 0:

  1. The character dies: it plays the death animation and lies on the ground.
  2. The attack and any skill being cast stop. All statuses come off, buffs included.
  3. Movement stops. Requests to move, attack or cast are ignored while dead.
  4. The death goes to the game log, and a kill by another player is logged as a PvP kill (see Game log and reports).
  5. Monsters forget the dead player and pick another target.

#The death screen

The HUD shows the death message with a countdown:

Waiting for a revive. Respawning in 13s

and a Respawn button. The button is grayed out for the first Respawn Choice Seconds (2 by default), then you can press it to come back at the spawn point at once instead of waiting. When the countdown reaches 0 you come back on your own.

#Coming back at the spawn point

Respawning, by the button or the countdown:

  1. Moves the player to the map's spawn point (a SpawnPoint in the map scene, at a random spot within its radius). A map with no spawn point uses one from another loaded scene, or the world origin.
  2. Takes the experience loss (below).
  3. Sets health to Respawn Health percent of the maximum (at least 1) and mana to Respawn Mana percent.
  4. Ends combat.
  5. Puts on the arrival protection.

The player stays on the same map. To send players to a town on death, change the spawn point of the map or override the behaviour in code.

#Experience loss

On coming back at the spawn point, the player loses Death Experience Loss percent (default 5) of the experience their current level needs:

lost = min(current experience, experience to next level × percent / 100)
  • It never takes a level away: at most, the bar goes to 0.
  • "-1,250 XP" appears over the character in red and the loss goes to the game log.
  • Revived by an ally: no loss.
  • Killed by another player (or by a player's summon): no loss, unless Pvp Death Costs Experience is on.
  • Duels never cost anything: a duel ends when a hit would kill, and the loser stays at 1 health. See PvP and duels.
  • Set the percent to 0 to turn the penalty off.

#Being revived

An ally can bring a fallen player back with a skill that has Targets Dead (targetsDead) and a Revive effect (see Skills). In the sample, the mage's Resurrect does this.

  • The skill only aims at fallen allies. Single target skills need the fallen ally clicked; area skills reach every fallen ally in the area and in sight.
  • The Revive effect sets how much health (default 30% at rank 1, +10% per rank) and mana (default 20%) the player comes back with.
  • The player stands up where they fell, with "Revived" over them in green, keeps their experience and gets the arrival protection.
  • The countdown stops. A player already back at the spawn point can't be revived.

#Protection after coming back

On entering a map, respawning or being revived, the player gets the Arrival Status (arrivalStatus) for Arrival Seconds (arrivalSeconds, default 4). Both are on the Game Database asset, section Invulnerability. The sample uses the "Protected" status: invulnerable, glowing, and it ends when the player attacks or casts. Leave Arrival Status empty for no protection.

#Settings

#Game Settings, Recovery

In KitARPG > Game Settings (the GameInstance on the KitNetwork prefab):

Field What it does Default
Combat Seconds (combatSeconds) Seconds a player stays in combat after the last hit they took or dealt. 6
Regen In Combat (regenInCombat) Health and mana keep regenerating in combat. Off, they wait until the fight is over. On
Out Of Combat Regen (outOfCombatRegen) Regeneration out of combat is multiplied by this. 3 recovers three times as fast between fights. 1
Level Up Restores Health (levelUpRestoresHealth) A new level fills health. On
Level Up Restores Mana (levelUpRestoresMana) A new level fills mana. On

#Game Settings, Death

Field What it does Default
Death Experience Loss (deathExperienceLoss) Percent of the current level's experience lost on coming back at the spawn point. 0 to 100. 5
Pvp Death Costs Experience (pvpDeathCostsExperience) Players killed by other players lose it too. Off
Respawn Health (respawnHealth) Percent of health a player comes back with at the spawn point. 1 to 100. 100
Respawn Mana (respawnMana) Percent of mana a player comes back with at the spawn point. 0 to 100. 100

#Player prefab

On the PlayerCharacter component of each player prefab (PlayerMale, PlayerFemale, PlayerPanda in the sample). Set the same values on every player prefab.

Field What it does Default
Respawn Seconds (respawnSeconds) Seconds lying down before coming back at the spawn point, unless an ally revives them first. 15
Respawn Choice Seconds (respawnChoiceSeconds) Seconds after dying before the Respawn button works. 2

#Game Database

Field What it does Default
Arrival Status (arrivalStatus) Status put on players when they appear in a map or come back to life. Should end on attacking or casting. Protected in the sample
Arrival Seconds (arrivalSeconds) How long it lasts. 4

#Map scene

Component Field What it does Default
SpawnPoint Radius Players appear at a random spot within this many meters. 2

#What the server checks

  • The Respawn request is refused while the player is alive, before Respawn Choice Seconds have passed, or after the player left the map. It also counts against the per-connection command pacing.
  • Revives only work on dead allies, never enemies, and only through a skill the caster can cast.
  • Regeneration, combat time and experience loss are only worked out on the server.

#Single player and MMO

The rules are the same. In single player your game is the host and runs these rules itself; the experience you keep after a death is saved with the character in the local save. In MMO, the map server sends the new experience to Central with the rest of the character. With Single Player Commands on (the default), /heal and /god work as cheats in single player (see GM tools).

#Step by step

#A forgiving game

  1. In Game Settings > Death, set Death Experience Loss to 0.
  2. In Recovery, set Out Of Combat Regen to 3 so players recover fast between fights.
  3. On each player prefab, lower Respawn Seconds to 5.

#A harsher game

  1. In Recovery, turn Regen In Combat off so potions and heals matter mid-fight.
  2. In Death, raise Death Experience Loss to 10 and set Respawn Health to 30 and Respawn Mana to 0.
  3. Turn on Pvp Death Costs Experience if PvP should be risky too.
  4. Turn off Level Up Restores Health and Level Up Restores Mana.

#Respawn in a safe place

  1. Open the map's scene and select its SpawnPoint (the sample calls it "Spawn").
  2. Move it to a safe spot on the NavMesh, away from monster spawners, and set its Radius.
  3. Keep one SpawnPoint per map scene. Players returning from death and new characters both appear there.

#Common mistakes

  • Players respawn at the world origin or in the wrong place. The map scene has no SpawnPoint. Add one on the NavMesh.
  • Players respawn inside a monster pack. The spawn point is near a spawner. Move one of them.
  • Health never regenerates. The class, attributes and gear give no healthRegen, or Out Of Combat Regen is 0, or Regen In Combat is off and the player is still being hit (a damage over time status keeps them in combat).
  • The Respawn button never works. Respawn Choice Seconds is larger than Respawn Seconds. Keep it smaller.
  • Different respawn times per character. Respawn Seconds is on each player prefab. Set the same value on all of them.
  • Players are invulnerable for too long after respawning. The Arrival Status doesn't end on action, or Arrival Seconds is long. The status needs Ends On Action on.
