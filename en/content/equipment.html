Equipment

Equipment is everything a character wears or holds: armor, accessories, weapons, shields, ammo and cosmetics. This page covers the equipment slots, every field of armor, weapons, weapon types and ammo, random bonuses rolled per copy, cosmetics, and how to make a weapon step by step.

#Slots

Every piece of gear has one slot (EquipSlot). The character has twelve gear positions, plus eight cosmetic positions over them.

Slot Position What goes there Shows on the body
Head 0 Hats, helmets, hoods, crowns Replaces the head region (and hides the hair, unless Keeps Hair)
Chest 1 Armor, robes, shirts Replaces the chest region
Hands 2 Gloves, gauntlets, bracers Replaces the hands region
Legs 3 Pants, skirts, greaves Replaces the legs region
Feet 4 Boots, shoes Replaces the feet region
MainHand 5 Weapons Held in the right hand bone (or left, for items marked Left Hand)
OffHand 6 Shields, off hand items, a second one-handed weapon whose type allows dual wield Held in the left hand bone
Ring 7 and 9 Rings. A character wears two: a ring fits either ring position. Not shown
Amulet 8 Necklaces and amulets Not shown
Ammo 10 Arrows, bolts Not shown
Back 11 Capes, packs, wings Added on the back (the body has nothing there)

Rules the server enforces when equipping:

  • The character must meet the item's Required Level and be one of its Classes (when the list isn't empty).
  • A two-handed weapon and an off hand item don't go together. Equipping one sends the other back to the bag; if the bag has no room, the equip is refused.
  • A one-handed weapon fits the off hand only when its weapon type has Dual Wield.
  • Cosmetic-only items go only in cosmetic positions.

Players equip by dragging from the bag to a slot, or with right click on an item in the bag; right click on a worn item takes it off. Worn gear still counts toward the bag's weight when bags are limited by weight. See Inventory and bank.

#Where gear is in the editor

Game Database > Items > Items has a subcategory per kind of gear: Weapons, Shields, Hats, Cloaks, Amulets, Rings, Chest, Gloves, Legs, Boots, Cosmetics and Ammo. A new entry made in one of them already has the right slot. You can also create assets with Create > KitARPG > Items > Armor, Weapon and Ammo.

The item page shows the tooltip as players see it, with random bonuses as ranges. Gear with fixed bonuses shows a card offering Make them roll (see random bonuses).

#Armor fields

Armor (EquipmentData) has every field items have, plus these, grouped in cards.

#Slot and cosmetic

Field What it does Default
Slot (slot) Where it is worn. Chest
Cosmetic (cosmetic) Only changes the look. Worn in the cosmetic position over its region, never gives stats, and goes nowhere else. Off

#Who can use it

Field What it does Default
Required Level (requiredLevel) Lowest character level that can wear it. The tooltip shows "Requires level N", in orange when the reader is below it. 1
Classes (classes) Classes that can wear or wield it. Empty means any class. The tooltip says "Your class can't use this" when it doesn't fit. Empty

#Bonuses

Field What it does Default
Random Bonuses (randomBonuses) Stats, attributes, damage and resistances, each with a minimum and a maximum. Every copy rolls its own value when it is made and keeps it. Same minimum and maximum never changes. See below. Empty
Bonuses (bonuses) Bonuses that never roll, like skill ranks. A full modifier bundle. Empty

#Look and wear

Field What it does Default
Model Variants (modelVariants) Other pieces for player models that need their own fit: Model Id (a player model's id) and Model. A character uses the variant for its model, or Model when there is none. Empty
Keeps Hair (keepsHair) Head gear that sits over the hair instead of replacing it, like a crown or a headband. Off
Max Durability (maxDurability) 0 means it never wears out. Each copy has a durability value saved with it; the kit's own systems don't lower it, so it is there for your own rules. 0
Set (set) The equipment set this piece belongs to. None

#Upgrades and sockets

Field What it does Default
Upgradable (upgradable) Can be upgraded at the forge (+1, +2...). On
Upgrade (upgrade) Its own upgrade rules. Empty uses the game database's. None
Sockets (sockets) How many gems it holds. 0
Salvage (salvage) What salvaging or breaking it gives. Empty uses the upgrade rules'. Empty

All of this is on Sets, upgrades and sockets.

#Held in hand

Field What it does Default
Left Hand (leftHand) Weapons and shields: the model is held by the left hand bone. The sample's bows use it. Off
Hold Position (holdPosition) Offset from the hand bone, in meters. 0, 0, 0
Hold Rotation (holdRotation) Rotation from the hand bone, in degrees. 0, 0, 0
Worn Effect (wornEffect) A looping effect while worn. Held gear carries it on its model, and particle systems shaped as a Mesh Renderer with no renderer set emit from the weapon's mesh (turn on Read/Write on the weapon mesh). Body gear carries it on its region's bone, one per hand or foot. None

#Weapons

A weapon (WeaponData) is armor in the main hand with damage and a weapon type. A new weapon starts in the MainHand slot with a stack of 1.

Field What it does Default
Weapon Type (weaponType) Sword, bow, staff... Sets range, speed, hands, ammo and attack effects. Skills can require it. None
Damages (damages) Rolls of a basic attack, one per element: Element (empty is the default element), Min and Max. Each roll is a hit of its own element. One neutral roll, 4 to 6

A basic attack rolls every entry between its min and max, adds the attacker's damage for that element, multiplies by power, takes off the defender's resistance and percent resistance for that element, and adds the elements up. A critical multiplies the total. The full formula is on Combat.

Upgrading a weapon grows its damage rolls by the upgrade's percent, rounded: a 10 to 14 sword at +20% hits 12 to 17.

Weapons are what skills scale with: a damage effect with Weapon Scale adds the weapon's rolls times that scale. See Skills.

#Weapon types

Weapon types are in Game Database > Combat > Weapon Types. Create one with Create > KitARPG > Items > Weapon Type.

Field What it does Default
Id, Display Name, Description, Icon As for every game data asset. The display name is the weapon's category in tooltips.
Two Handed (twoHanded) Takes both hands, so nothing goes in the off hand. Off
Dual Wield (dualWield) One-handed weapons of this type can also go in the off hand. Basic attacks then take turns between both hands; skills use the main hand. Off
Uses Ammo (usesAmmo) Each basic attack, and each skill that uses the weapon attack, spends one ammo from the ammo slot. With no fitting ammo the weapon can't attack. Off
Attack Range (attackRange) Distance of the basic attack, in meters. 2
Attacks Per Second (attacksPerSecond) Basic attacks per second before the character's attack speed. 1
Effects (effects) The basic attack's effects: Launch Effect on the attacker at each hit (a swing trail or flash), Projectile (prefab, the socket it leaves from and its speed in meters per second; damage lands when it arrives) and Hit Effect on whoever is hit (empty uses the element's). Launch effect on the right hand, no projectile

The attack animation for each weapon type is set per character, in the player model's Character Animations. See Animations.

The sample has 16 weapon types:

Type Hands Range Attacks per second Other
Sword One 2 1.2 Dual wield
Greatsword Two 2.5 0.8
Spear Two 3 1
Bow Two 12 1 Uses ammo, arrow projectile
Axe One 2 1.1 Dual wield
Mace One 2 0.9
Halberd Two 3.2 0.8
Scythe Two 2.8 0.85
Crossbow Two 14 0.7 Uses ammo, arrow projectile
Wand One 9 1.1 Orb projectile
Staff Two 11 0.8 Orb projectile
Scepter One 9 1 Orb projectile
Tome One 10 0.9 Orb projectile
Orb One 10 1 Orb projectile
Dagger One 1.6 1.5 Dual wield
Fist One 1.6 1.4 Dual wield

#Ammo

Ammo (AmmoData) is gear worn in the ammo slot. A new ammo item starts with a stack of 500, weight 0 and Upgradable off.

Field What it does Default
Weapon Types (weaponTypes) Weapon types that shoot it. Empty means any type with Uses Ammo. Empty

Every other armor field applies. Ammo bonuses count while it is worn, so fire arrows can add fire damage to every shot. The sample has Arrows and Fire Arrows (+4 Fire damage) for bows, and Bolts and Frost Bolts (+4 Water damage) for crossbows. Hunters start with 200 Arrows worn.

Warning

Ammo stacks, so it must not have random bonuses: a copy with its own rolls can't share a stack. Put ammo bonuses in Bonuses. Project Check warns about any gear that stacks and rolls, and offers Make bonuses fixed.

#Random bonuses rolled per copy

Every bonus on gear can have a minimum and a maximum. When a copy is made (dropped as loot, bought, given as a start item, a quest reward), the server rolls a whole number between them for each bonus, and that copy keeps its values for good. Two Knight Helms are rarely the same.

#Fields of a random bonus

Field What it does Default
Target (target) What it raises: Stat (flat), Stat Percent (percent of the stat's total), Damage (flat element damage), Resistance (flat element resistance), Resistance Percent or Attribute (points). Stat
Stat (stat) The stat, picked from a list. Only for Stat and Stat Percent. Empty
Element (element) Only for Damage, Resistance and Resistance Percent. Empty is the default element. None
Attribute (attribute) Only for Attribute. None
Min (min) Lowest value, a whole number. 0
Max (max) Highest value. If you swap min and max, the kit uses the lower one as the minimum. 0

Percents count in whole points: 5 is 5%. That covers Stat Percent, Resistance Percent and Stat on a stat shown as a percent (critical, power, attack speed and so on): a Stat roll on critChance from 1 to 3 gives 1% to 3% critical.

#What players see

  • A copy's tooltip shows its value: "+7 Health". Where the tooltip shows ranges, it adds the range: "+7 Health (3-9)".
  • Without a copy (in a shop, in the Game Database), it shows the range: "+(3-9) Health".
  • A bonus with the same min and max shows as a plain "+5 Health".

#How rolls work, step by step

  1. The item's Random Bonuses list is read in order.
  2. For each entry, a whole number from min to max (both included) is picked.
  3. The values are saved with the copy, by position in the list.
  4. While the copy is worn, each value is added to the item's fixed Bonuses, then the upgrade percent (if any) grows the total, fixed and rolled bonuses alike.
  5. Gems in the copy's sockets add their own bonuses, and set bonuses are added once per set.

If you change an item's ranges later, saved copies keep their values, pulled into the new range if they fall outside it. Copies made before the item had rolls get fresh values when they are loaded. Because values are stored by position, add new bonuses at the end of the list; reordering moves values to other bonuses.

Copies with rolls never stack. When a character or bank is loaded, loose copies of an item that now stacks (for example ammo saved back when it had rolls) are merged into stacks.

#Turning fixed bonuses into rolls

A piece with fixed bonuses shows a card on its Game Database page: "N fixed bonuses that never roll. Turn them into min and max ranges, 25% up and down?" with Make them roll. It turns each fixed stat, percent, damage, resistance and attribute into a range around its value, 25% up and down (8 becomes 6 to 10; values below 2 don't spread). A bonus to something already in Random Bonuses widens that one; the rest go at the end. Skill ranks can't roll, so they stay in Bonuses.

The sample's gear was built this way: every fixed bonus became a range when the sample was built.

The reverse, Make bonuses fixed in Project Check, turns each range into its middle value and clears Random Bonuses. Use it for items that stack.

#Cosmetics

A cosmetic changes how a region looks without touching stats: a player keeps wearing a hat with good bonuses, and shows another hat over it.

  • There are 8 cosmetic positions, over Head, Chest, Hands, Legs, Feet, Back, MainHand and OffHand.
  • Any piece for that region can go in its cosmetic position, even gear with stats; there it counts only for its look.
  • Items with Cosmetic on go only in cosmetic positions. Equipping one from the bag puts it in its cosmetic position.
  • Class and level rules still apply.
  • Cosmetic positions give nothing: no stats, no set pieces, no conditions.
  • A cosmetic weapon shows only over a real weapon of the same weapon type, so attacks still match the animation.

In the game, the character panel of the bag has Show cosmetics / Show gear to switch the slots between gear and looks, and the bag has a Cosmetics tab. In the Game Database, cosmetics have their own subcategory.

The sample sells costumes and outfits at the hatter: Cat Hood, Bunny Hood, Witch Hat, Pirate Coat, Fairy Wings Costume, the Snowman and Skeleton outfits, Party Balloons and Gift Box.

#Make a weapon

This example makes a Storm Spear: a level 8 two-handed spear for warriors and hunters that hits with neutral and air damage, rolls a bit of air damage and agility, and holds one gem.

  1. Open Game Database > Items > Weapons and press + New weapon; pick Spear from the weapon types. Type storm_spear in Id and Storm Spear in Display Name, and write a Description.
  2. Set Rarity to Rare, Sell Price to 60 and Weight to 6. Leave Max Stack at 1.
  3. In Damages, set the first roll to Neutral 5 to 8, and add a second roll: Air 6 to 9.
  4. In Who can use it, set Required Level to 8, and add Warrior and Hunter to Classes.
  5. In Bonuses, add two Random Bonuses:
    • Target Damage, Element Air, Min 1, Max 3.
    • Target Attribute, Attribute Agility, Min 0, Max 3.
  6. Put fixed bonuses in Bonuses only if they should never roll, like +1 rank to Charge in Skills.
  7. In Upgrades and sockets, set Sockets to 1. Leave Upgrade empty to use the database's rules.
  8. In Look and wear and Held in hand, set Model to the spear's model and adjust Hold Position and Hold Rotation until it sits in the hand. Play and look at it in the bag's character portrait.
  9. Run KitARPG > Render Missing Item Icons for the icon.
  10. Add it to a monster's loot table or a shop. In the bag, the tooltip shows "5 to 8 neutral damage", "6 to 9 air damage", then "+(1-3) Air damage" and "+(0-3) Agility" until a copy exists.

To make the spear usable by any class, leave Classes empty. To make skills require spears, add the Spear type to the skill's Weapons.

#Make a piece of armor

  1. Open Game Database > Items > Chest and press + New chest piece. Set Id and Display Name.
  2. Set Model to a model that holds a mesh named <Name>_Chest, rigged to your bodies' skeleton. For other bodies, add Model Variants entries with their model ids.
  3. Set Required Level, Classes, Random Bonuses and Sockets as for a weapon.
  4. Set Set if it belongs to an equipment set.

#Single player and MMO

Gear rules are the same in both modes and run on the server. Each copy's rolls, upgrade level and gems travel to its owner for the tooltip. Other players only receive the item id at each position, which is all they need to draw the gear.

  • In MMO, rolls, upgrade level and gems are saved in the copy's row of item_instances, in its Extra column.
  • In single player, they are saved inside the character's JSON.

#Tips

  • Give chest pieces bigger rolls than gloves. The sample scales each piece's bonuses by its size: a chest gives three times what gloves do.
  • Keep Min above 0 for the bonus that defines an item, so no copy feels empty.
  • Use Classes on gear, never a list of weapons on classes: one place to edit per item.

#Common mistakes

  • Two copies of an item won't stack. The item has random bonuses. Stacking gear must use fixed bonuses.
  • Rolled values moved to the wrong bonus. Entries in Random Bonuses were reordered or one in the middle was removed. Add new ones at the end.
  • A bow can't attack. Its type uses ammo and no fitting ammo is worn. Check the ammo's Weapon Types.
  • A dagger won't go in the off hand. The Dagger weapon type needs Dual Wield, and the dagger must not be two-handed.
