Reference

Keys

Every default key and mouse action in KitARPG, per control mode, plus the keys that work in windows and where to change each one. Players can't rebind keys in game; you set them on the components in the HUD prefab and the mode components.

#How keys work in the kit

  • The kit reads keys through KitInput, which uses the Input System package when the project has it on, and the old Input Manager otherwise. Every key below works in both.
  • Game keys are ignored while the player types in a text field (chat, search, amounts).
  • With Game Settings > Input Layout (inputLayout) set to Touch, game keys do nothing; touch controls take over. Touch And Keys keeps both. See Touch controls.
  • Ctrl on Windows is Cmd on Mac wherever a modifier is listed as Ctrl/Cmd.

#Keys in every mode

Key Action Set on
1 to 9, 0 Use the skill or item on that quick bar slot. An area skill first shows its shape PlayerHud > Hotbar Keys (hotbarKeys)
F Pick up or talk to what is nearest (within 4 meters by default) The mode's input component (interactKey)
Enter Open the chat box. In the box, Enter sends Chat window
Esc Close the top window, cancel aiming, or open the game menu when nothing is open Fixed
I Bag and equipment InventoryWindow > Toggle Key (toggleKey)
C Character: attributes and stats CharacterWindow > toggleKey
K Skills SkillWindow > toggleKey
L Quest log QuestLogWindow > toggleKey
M World map WorldMapWindow > toggleKey
B Bestiary BestiaryWindow > toggleKey
O Friends FriendsWindow > toggleKey
G Guild GuildWindow > toggleKey
N Mail MailWindow > toggleKey
P In-game shop (MMO only) CashShopWindow > toggleKey
F8 GM panel, for staff accounts only GmWindow > toggleKey
Mouse wheel Zoom the camera, unless the pointer is over the interface Mode camera

A window whose module is off in Modules doesn't open with its key.

#Top-down mode

The default mode: a fixed camera above the player, Diablo style.

Input Action
Left click on the ground Walk there. Hold the button to keep walking toward the pointer
Left click on a monster Walk into range and attack it
Left click on loot Walk to it and pick it up
Left click on an NPC Walk to it and talk
Right click on another player Open the player menu
W A S D or arrow keys Walk, relative to the camera
Space Attack the nearest enemy within 10 meters. Hold to keep attacking
F Pick up or talk to the nearest thing within 4 meters
Mouse wheel Zoom between 5 and 18 meters

Players choose how they walk in Settings > Controls > Movement: Click and keys (default), Click only or Keys only.

Fields on TopDownInput, on each map's camera:

Field What it does Default
Controls (controls) Click and keys, click only, or keys only, when players don't choose Click and keys
Player Chooses (playerChooses) Adds Movement to Settings > Controls On
Attack Key (attackKey) Attacks the nearest enemy Space
Interact Key (interactKey) Picks up or talks F
Key Attack Range (keyAttackRange) Meters in which the attack key looks for an enemy 10
Key Interact Range (keyInteractRange) Meters in which the interact key finds loot or an NPC 4
Player Menu Button (playerMenuButton) Mouse button for the player menu: 0 left, 1 right, 2 middle 1

#Free camera mode

The cursor aims; the camera orbits behind the player.

Input Action
W A S D or arrow keys Walk, relative to the camera
Left click Swing toward the pointer. Hold to keep swinging. Nothing needs a target: the server finds who is hit
Right button held Turn the camera
Right click without moving the mouse, on another player Open the player menu
Space Dodge
F Pick up or talk to the nearest thing within 4 meters
Mouse wheel Zoom within the camera's range

#Third person mode

The cursor is hidden and the camera turns with the mouse; a crosshair aims.

Input Action
Mouse Turn the camera
W A S D or arrow keys Walk, relative to the camera
Left click Swing at what the crosshair covers. Hold to keep swinging
Right click on another player Open the player menu
Space Dodge
F Pick up or talk to the nearest thing within 4 meters
Left Alt held Show the cursor, to click the interface
Mouse wheel Zoom within the camera's range

The cursor also comes back by itself while a window, dialog or the game menu is open.

Players set mouse sensitivity, invert the mouse, and Turn with the camera (the player faces where the camera looks) in Settings > Controls.

Fields on the third person components:

Component Field What it does Default
ThirdPersonInput Dodge Key (dodgeKey) Dodge Space
ThirdPersonInput Interact Key (interactKey) Pick up or talk F
ThirdPersonInput Interact Range (interactRange) Meters for the interact key 4
ThirdPersonCamera Look Button (lookButton) Free camera: mouse button held to turn 1 (right)
ThirdPersonCamera Free Cursor Key (freeCursorKey) Third person: held to get the cursor back Left Alt

#Aiming skills

In every mode, an area skill shows its circle, cone or line where you aim before it is cast.

Input Action
Left click, or the skill's key again Cast
Right click or Esc Cancel

Players can turn on Cast at once in Settings > Controls to skip the preview.

#Bag, equipment and items

Input Action
Drag an item Move, stack, swap or wear it
Double click an item in the bag Use, wear, or do what the window beside it does (deposit at the bank, offer in a trade, sell)
Double click a worn item Take it off
Shift + double click a stack Split the stack in half, into the first free slot
Right click an item Open the item menu
Hover or click an item Show its details, as Game Settings > Item Details On Hover says

When moving part of a stack asks "how many?":

Input Action
Hold Shift while moving Move one, without asking
Hold Ctrl/Cmd while moving Move the whole stack, without asking
Shift on the amount arrows Steps of 10 instead of 1
Enter Confirm
Esc or a click outside Close

#NPC shops

Input Action
Right click an item for sale Buy one
Shift + right click, or Shift + the buy button Buy a stack
Right click in the buyback list Buy back
Esc, or walking away Close

#Character window

Input Action
+ next to an attribute Add one point. Nothing is sent until you confirm
Shift + + Add every point that fits

#Chat

Input Action
Enter Open the chat box, or send what you typed
Esc in the box Clear and leave the box
The channel button Cycle the default channel
/s, /m, /g, /p, /gu, /al alone Switch the default channel to say, map, world, party, guild or alliance
Right click a player's name Open the player menu

Every chat command is in GM commands.

#Dialogs and other windows

Input Action
Enter Yes, in a confirmation dialog
Esc No, or close the window
Mouse wheel over the minimap Zoom the minimap. Its + and - buttons do the same
Walking away from an NPC, loot or a shop Closes its window

#Touch controls

With touch controls on, the screen replaces the keys:

Control Action
Stick on the left Walk
Attack Attack (top-down: the nearest enemy; third person: what the crosshair covers or the nearest enemy)
Skill buttons 1 to 4 around Attack Tap to cast at the nearest enemy in range; drag to aim and release to cast; drop on the X to cancel
Button 5 at the top left A consumable, such as a potion
Hold a skill button, or tap an empty one Pick what it holds
Activate on the right edge Appears near loot or an NPC; does what F does
Drag on the world Turn the camera (third person and free camera)
Pinch Zoom
Tap on the world What a click does

There is no dodge button on screen. See Touch controls.

#Changing keys

  1. Open the HUD prefab (Game Settings > Interface > Hud Prefab, GameHud.prefab in the sample).
  2. Find the window under Windows, for example Inventory Window, and change Toggle Key.
  3. For the quick bar, select the PlayerHud and edit Hotbar Keys.
  4. For movement, attack, dodge and interact keys, edit the mode components on each map's main camera (TopDownInput, ThirdPersonInput, ThirdPersonCamera).
  5. Save the prefab or scene and rebuild your clients.
Tip

Change the HUD prefab rather than the sample's original so kit updates don't fight your changes. See HUD and windows.
