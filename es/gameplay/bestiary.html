Bestiary

Esta página aún no está traducida. Se muestra en inglés.

The bestiary is an in-game book of every monster: its level, rank and behaviour, its characteristics, where it lives, and what it drops with the chance the player has for each item. This page covers what players see, how the bestiary finds its data, and how to keep it complete.

#For the player

Press B or click the bestiary button in the icon bar at the top right.

#The list

On the left, every monster of the game, sorted by level and then by name, with a count like "12 monsters" on top. Each row shows the monster's portrait, its name in the color of its rank, where it lives and its level.

Three filters sit above the list:

Filter What it does
Search Keeps monsters whose name contains the text
Map All maps or one map: keeps monsters that live there
Rank All ranks, Normal, Elite or Boss

#The monster's page

Click a row to see it on the right:

  • The portrait and the name.
  • A line like Level 12 · Elite · Aggressive. The behaviour is Passive, Aggressive or Defends its pack.
  • The experience it gives you, worked out with your level the way the server does, and the maps where it lives: 340 XP for you · Forest, Sunken Crypt.

Two tabs:

Characteristics: health, damage by element, attacks per second and reach, its other stats, resistances by element (negative ones in orange), immunities to statuses, its skills, and a boss's rage.

Drops:

  • Gold per kill (with the server's gold rate) and the most items one kill can drop: 12 to 30 gold · up to 2 items a kill.
  • Each item with its icon (hover it for the item tooltip), the amount, and the chance you have to get it. Your chance is the table's chance raised by your Loot Chance stat and the server's loot rate, as the server rolls it. When it differs from the table's, the base chance shows too.
  • Quest drops say which quest they are for, and their chance isn't raised.
  • "Drops nothing." for a monster without a loot table.
Nota

A loot event started with /rates isn't counted in the chances shown. The client doesn't know about it. See GM tools.

#Where the data comes from

The bestiary reads everything from the Game Database, so it works with no server and shows the same in single player and MMO.

What From
Which monsters Every monster in the Game Database with In Bestiary on
Portrait The monster's Icon
Characteristics and drops The monster and its loot table
Where it lives Each map's Monsters list (MapData.monsters)
Experience The game's combat formula with the player's level

#In Bestiary

Field What it does Default
In Bestiary (inBestiary) Listed in the bestiary. Turn off for helpers like tax collectors or summons nobody hunts On

#Where each monster lives

Each map in the Game Database has a Monsters list. You don't need to fill it by hand:

  1. Open the map's card in Game Database > Maps.
  2. Press Read from scene. It reads the MonsterSpawners of the map's scene, plus the monsters its bosses call.
  3. Save. The sample fills these lists when it is built.

Do it again after adding or removing spawners.

#Portraits

KitARPG > Render Missing Monster Icons takes a three-quarter portrait of every monster without an icon, from its prefab, and sets it as its icon. The sample does it when it is built. To redo one, clear its icon and run the menu again.

#Step by step: add a new monster to the bestiary

  1. Create the monster in the Game Database and leave In Bestiary on. See Monsters.
  2. Give it a loot table.
  3. Place a MonsterSpawner for it in a map scene.
  4. On that map's card, press Read from scene.
  5. Run KitARPG > Render Missing Monster Icons.
  6. Press Play and open the bestiary with B.

#Single player and MMO

The bestiary works the same in both. The chances use the rates in Game Settings > Rates (experienceRate, lootRate, goldRate), which the server also uses.

Turn it off with the Bestiary module in Modules. The button and the key go away.

#In code

BestiaryWindow is on the HUD prefab. Its methods Matches (filters), Characteristics (the first tab) and ChanceFor (the chance shown) are virtual, so a subclass can add filters or show data of your own. The key is the Toggle Key field (toggleKey, B). See Overriding kit behaviour.

#Common mistakes

  • A monster doesn't say where it lives, or the map filter skips it. Its map's Monsters list is out of date. Press Read from scene on the map.
  • A monster has no portrait. It has no icon. Run Render Missing Monster Icons.
  • The tax collector shows in the bestiary. Turn off its In Bestiary.
  • Chances don't match a GM event. Events from /rates aren't shown.
