Touch controls

Esta página aún no está traducida. Se muestra en inglés.

Phones and tablets play KitARPG with on-screen controls: a stick, an Attack button, skill and potion buttons, an Activate button and touches on the world. This page covers when they appear, how they play in each control mode, every component and field on the prefab, and how to change the layout.

#What the touch controls are

The touch controls are a canvas made from a prefab, TouchControls.prefab, created once when the game starts and kept between maps, like the HUD. While they're in use, the control modes read the stick, the buttons and the touches on the world instead of the mouse and keys. They work in all three control modes.

The mouse is ignored while they're in use. On a phone Unity moves a simulated mouse under the first finger, which would otherwise walk the character under the stick.

#Turning them on

Open KitARPG > Game Settings, section Interface.

Field What it does Default
Input Layout (inputLayout) What the game is played with (table below). Auto
Touch Controls Prefab (touchControlsPrefab) Canvas with the on-screen controls. Empty: no touch controls. Sample: Samples/Starter/Prefabs/TouchControls.prefab
Input Layout What it does
Auto Touch controls on phones and tablets, the Device Simulator included. Mouse and keys everywhere else.
Mouse And Keys Never shows the touch controls.
Touch Always shows the touch controls. The game's keys do nothing: walking, attack, dodge, interact and the hotbar keys.
Touch And Keys Shows the touch controls and keeps the keyboard working. For tablets with a keyboard, and to try the touch controls in the editor.

The layout is decided when the game starts: changing it in Play mode has no effect until the next Play.

Nota

The touch controls are a client feature. The server checks touch players exactly as it checks mouse players, so single player and MMO behave the same. Server builds never create them.

#Trying them in the editor

  1. Set Input Layout to Touch And Keys.
  2. Press Play and log in. The controls appear once your character is in the world.
  3. The mouse acts as a finger: drag the stick, click the buttons, drag on the world.

Or keep Auto and switch the Game view to the Simulator with a phone selected: the game then counts as mobile.

#Playing with touch

#The sample layout

Piece Where What it does
Move stick Bottom left Walks. It appears where your thumb lands anywhere in its area and goes home when you let go. Faded while untouched.
Chat button Left, above the stick Shows or puts away the chat. The chat starts put away; a red badge counts lines that came in meanwhile (99+ past 99).
Attack Bottom right, large Attacks (see the mode tables below).
Skill 1 to 4 In an arc around Attack: 1 low on the left, 2 on the left, 3 and 4 above Hotbar slots 1 to 4. They show the skill's icon, the cooldown running down and its seconds. Greyed out when not learned or without mana.
Consumable Top left of the arc Hotbar slot 5, for consumables such as a potion. Shows how many are left in the bag.
Activate Right edge, above the arc Appears only with loot or an NPC within 4 m. Picks up the nearest loot or talks to the nearest NPC, the same as F.
X (cancel area) Above skill 4 Appears only while you drag from a skill button. Letting go over it calls the cast off.
The world Everywhere else Touches, drags and pinches (see below).

The controls hide while a window, the chat input, a dialog, the settings, the game menu or the loading screen is open, and come back when it closes. Their canvas sits under the HUD, so game windows cover them.

There is no dodge button in the sample layout. With Touch alone, free camera and third person players can't dodge until you add one (see Adding a button).

#Skill and consumable buttons

  • Tap a skill: casts it at the enemy the crosshair already covers (third person), or else at the nearest enemy within the skill's range plus 2 m. With no enemy around, it goes ahead of the character, up to 4 m. Self and ally skills cast the usual way.
  • Drag from a skill button: aims instead. The drag maps to the ground around the character as the camera sees it; 160 pixels (at 1080p) of drag reach the skill's full range. The skill's area preview follows. Let go to cast there.
  • Cancel a drag: let go over the X, or back on the button (less than 24 pixels from where you started).
  • Tap a consumable: uses it. Dragging a consumable shows the X too; letting go elsewhere uses it.
  • Hold a button still for 0.6 seconds, or tap an empty one: opens the picker.

The touch buttons are the same hotbar the keys use on a computer. Slot 1 on a phone is slot 1 on a desktop, so a character keeps its setup across devices. The client checks learned, cooldown, casting and mana before sending, and the server checks everything again (see Skills).

#The slot picker

The picker is a panel over the game with the title "Choose a skill" (or "Choose a consumable") and a grid of icons:

  • Skill buttons list every skill the character can cast: the class skills learned so far plus skills learned outside the class. Passive skills are left out.
  • The consumable button lists the consumables in the bag, with the total of each.
  • The entry the button holds now has a gold ring.
  • Picking an entry that sits on another slot swaps the two, so nothing shows twice.
  • Empty this button clears it. X or Escape closes the picker.

#Top-down with touch

Touch What happens
Stick Walks in that direction, relative to the camera, exactly like WASD.
A finger down on the world What a click does: attacks the monster or enemy player there, picks up loot, talks to an NPC, or walks there.
Keep the finger on the world Keeps walking toward it. A second finger stops the walk and turns it into a pinch.
Touch another player who isn't an enemy Opens the player menu (there is no right button).
Attack Attacks the nearest enemy within 10 m. Held, it moves on when the current one falls.
Pinch Zooms.

#Free camera and third person with touch

Touch What happens
Stick Walks relative to the camera. Pushed halfway, it walks at half speed.
Drag one finger on the world Turns the camera, within what its Camera Settings allow. In third person the crosshair stays on the whole time.
Tap the world Picks up the loot, talks to the NPC or tax collector there; otherwise opens the menu on the player there.
Attack Swings at what the crosshair covers (third person) or else at the nearest enemy within attack range plus 2 m. Held, it keeps swinging.
Pinch Zooms within the camera's zoom range.

In free camera on a touch screen there is no pointer, so swings and skills find their own aim: the nearest enemy, or ahead.

#The prefab

KitARPG > Starter > Build Scenes and Prefabs creates Samples/Starter/Prefabs/TouchControls.prefab when it's missing and assigns it to Touch Controls Prefab if that field is empty. An existing prefab is never replaced, so your edits survive a rebuild of the sample.

Its pieces are nested prefabs in Samples/Starter/Prefabs/UI:

Prefab Used for
Touch Stick The move stick
Touch Button Attack and Activate
Touch Skill Button The four skill buttons and the consumable button
Touch Slot Picker The picker

Change Touch Skill Button once and all five buttons follow. Open TouchControls.prefab to move, resize, restyle or delete any piece.

The root canvas scales with screen size from a 1920 by 1080 reference, matching width and height half and half, and sorts at order 9, under the HUD.

Consejo

Prefabs made by older kit versions are rearranged by the kit update Touch controls: skills 1 to 4 and potion on 5, listed in Overview. It places skills 1 to 4 and the potion around Attack and removes the old Dodge button. See Updating the kit.

#Components

All components are in Runtime/Client/Touch (namespace Blunnyn.KitARPG.Client).

#TouchControls

On the root of the prefab. It collects the input of every piece and fits the desktop HUD around the controls.

Field What it does Default
Group (group) Canvas group that hides the controls while a window, the chat or a dialog is open. Empty: the one on the same object. The root's
Look Speed (lookSpeed) How fast dragging on the world turns the camera. Multiplied by the camera's sensitivity and the player's mouse sensitivity. 1
Pinch Speed (pinchSpeed) Meters of zoom per 100 pixels of pinch. 1.5
Hud Changes (hudChanges) How the mouse and keys HUD is fitted around the controls (below). Sample list

Each Hud Change finds a piece of the HUD by its path under the HUD prefab and changes it while the touch controls are in use. Everything goes back when they're turned off.

Field What it does Default
Path (path) Path under the HUD, such as HUD/Chat Window/Chat. A path that isn't found logs a warning. Empty
Hide (hide) Hidden while the touch controls are in use. Off
Move (move) Applies the anchor, pivot, position and scale below. Off
Anchor (anchor) Anchor point of the moved piece. (0.5, 0.5)
Pivot (pivot) Pivot of the moved piece. (0.5, 0.5)
Position (position) Anchored position. (0, 0)
Scale (scale) Scale, at least 0.1. 1

The sample list hides the desktop hotbar slots Hotbar 1 to Hotbar 10 and the Hint line, moves the player HUD to the bottom center at 85 percent, and moves the chat to the left above the Chat button at 75 percent. If you rename or move pieces in your HUD, update these paths. See HUD and windows.

#TouchStick

A thumb stick that writes an axis from -1 to 1 on each side.

Field What it does Default
Axis (axis) Axis it writes. The modes walk with Move. Move
Background (background) The ring the knob moves in. Set in the prefab
Knob (knob) The knob. Set in the prefab
Radius (radius) How far the knob goes from the middle, in canvas units. 90 (92 in the sample)
Dead Zone (deadZone) Share of the radius that counts as no push, 0 to 0.9. Past it the push starts from zero, so a small tilt walks slowly. 0.15
Floating (floating) The stick appears where the thumb lands instead of staying put. On
Idle Alpha (idleAlpha) Opacity while untouched. 0.5

Touching anywhere inside the stick object's rectangle grabs it. In the sample that area is 560 by 480 at the bottom left.

#TouchButton

A button the modes read by its id.

Field What it does Default
Id (id) Attack, Dodge, Interact, or an id your own code reads. Attack
Show (show) Always, or Near Interactable: only with loot or an NPC within Near Range. Always
Near Range (nearRange) Meters for Near Interactable. 4
Face (face) The graphic that shrinks while held. Set in the prefab
Pressed Scale (pressedScale) Scale of the face while held, 0.5 to 1. 0.9
On Press (onPress) Event raised when pressed, for hooking a method without code that reads the id. Empty

What each id does:

Id Top-down Free camera and third person
Attack Attacks the nearest enemy within 10 m; held, moves on to the next Swings at the crosshair's enemy or the nearest in reach; held, keeps swinging
Dodge Nothing Dodges
Interact Picks up or talks to the nearest thing within 4 m Same

#TouchSkillButton

Casts the skill or uses the item in one hotbar slot.

Field What it does Default
Slot (slot) Hotbar slot, from 0. Slot 0 is the 1 key on a computer. 0
Holds (holds) Skills, Consumables or Anything. Anything else in the slot shows as empty. Skills
Icon (icon) Image that shows the skill or item. Set in the prefab
Cooldown (cooldown) Filled image that runs down with the cooldown. Set in the prefab
Cooldown Label (cooldownLabel) Seconds left: one decimal under 10, whole seconds above. Set in the prefab
Amount Label (amountLabel) How many of the item are left in the bag. Set in the prefab
Cancel Area (cancelArea) Shown while dragging; letting go over it does nothing. The X in the sample
Face (face) The graphic that shrinks while held. Set in the prefab
Aim Radius (aimRadius) Pixels at 1080p of drag that reach the skill's full range. 160
Aim Start (aimStart) Pixels at 1080p of drag before the touch aims instead of tapping. 24
Tap Reach (tapReach) Meters past the skill's range in which a tap still finds an enemy. 2
Pick Seconds (pickSeconds) Seconds held still that open the picker. 0: only an empty button opens it. 0.6
Unusable (unusable) Icon tint when the skill can't be cast or the item is gone. Grey

#TouchSlotPicker

The panel that fills skill buttons. Each entry is a copy of the template button.

Field What it does Default
Panel (panel) Shown while choosing. Give it a backdrop so touches don't reach the game. Set in the prefab
Title (title) "Choose a skill" or "Choose a consumable". Set in the prefab
List (list) Where the entries go, usually a grid. Set in the prefab
Entry Template (entryTemplate) Button copied per entry. Needs a child Icon image and a Name label; Amount is optional. Set in the prefab
Current Color (currentColor) Color of the entry the button holds now. Gold
Clear Button (clearButton) Empties the button. Empty this button
Close Button (closeButton) Closes the picker. X

#TouchChatButton

Field What it does Default
Start Hidden (startHidden) The chat starts put away. On
Badge (badge) Shown while there are unread lines. Set in the prefab
Badge Label (badgeLabel) The count. Set in the prefab
Face (face) Image that turns lighter while the chat is shown. Set in the prefab
Open Color (openColor) Face color while the chat is shown. Light gold

When the touch controls go away, the chat comes back as on a desktop.

#TouchLookArea

A transparent image covering the whole screen, behind every other control. It turns touches on the world into input:

  • One finger dragging writes the Look axis (turns the camera in free camera and third person).
  • Two fingers pinching write the Zoom axis (zoom in every mode).
  • A short touch is a tap.
  • A finger resting on the world is a press, which top-down walks toward.
Field What it does Default
Tap Travel (tapTravel) Pixels at 1080p a finger may move and still count as a tap. 18
Tap Seconds (tapSeconds) Seconds a touch may last and still count as a tap. 0.35

#TouchModes

Keeps a piece only in some control modes. Put it on a button that makes no sense in a mode, such as Dodge in top-down.

Field What it does Default
Modes (modes) Modes that show this piece: TopDown, FreeCamera, ThirdPerson. Empty: all of them. Empty

#Changing the layout

#Moving or resizing a button

  1. Open Samples/Starter/Prefabs/TouchControls.prefab.
  2. Select the piece under Actions (Attack, Activate, the skill buttons, the X) or at the root (Move Stick, Chat Button).
  3. Move or resize its Rect Transform. Keep buttons at least about 100 units across at the 1920 by 1080 reference so thumbs hit them.

#Adding a button

To give free camera and third person players a dodge button:

  1. Open TouchControls.prefab.
  2. Drag Prefabs/UI/Touch Button under Actions and place it.
  3. Set its Id to Dodge and its label to "Dodge".
  4. Add a TouchModes component with Modes FreeCamera and ThirdPerson, so it hides in top-down.

#Adding a skill button

  1. Duplicate one of the skill buttons under Actions.
  2. Set Slot to the next hotbar slot (5 for the 6th slot) and Holds as you like.
  3. Set its Cancel Area to the X.

#A button for your own code

Give a TouchButton an id of your own and read it, or hook a method to On Press:

if (TouchControls.Down("Jump")) Jump();      // pressed this frame
if (TouchControls.Held("Block")) Block();    // held
Vector2 move = TouchControls.Axis("Move");   // the stick

Check GameInstance.KeysOn before reading keys of your own, so they stay off with Touch alone. See Game modes in code.

#Common mistakes

  • Touch controls don't show in the editor. Input Layout is Auto and the Game view isn't the Simulator. Use Touch And Keys, or the Simulator.
  • Touch controls don't show on a phone. Touch Controls Prefab is empty, or Input Layout is Mouse And Keys.
  • A HUD piece overlaps the controls after you edited the HUD. A Hud Change path no longer matches. Look for "Touch controls: no ... in the HUD" in the Console and fix the path.
  • No way to dodge on a phone. The sample has no dodge button; add one as shown above.
  • Skill buttons stay empty. They show what the hotbar holds. Hold a button or tap it empty to pick a skill, or put skills on hotbar slots 1 to 4 on a computer first.
  • The keyboard stops working. Input Layout is Touch, which turns the game keys off by design. Use Touch And Keys for tablets with a keyboard.
  • Changing the layout in Play mode. The layout is read when the game starts. Stop and press Play again.
