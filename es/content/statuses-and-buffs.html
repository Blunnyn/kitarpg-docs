Statuses and buffs

Esta página aún no está traducida. Se muestra en inglés.

Statuses are buffs and debuffs that last a while: poison, burn, stun, slow, root, silence, shields, regeneration, invisibility, invulnerability. This page covers every field of a status, stacking, damage and healing over time, shields, immunities and resistances, the plain stat buffs skills give, and how to make a status step by step.

#What a status is

A status is an asset defined once and applied by anything: a skill (Apply Status effect), a consumable (Status item effect), a boss phase, the arrival protection, or your own code. While it lasts, it can:

  • Stop its holder from doing things (stun, root, silence, disarm) or protect it (invisible, invulnerable, unstoppable).
  • Change stats: any modifier bundle, per stack. A slow is a negative move speed percent.
  • Deal damage, heal or change mana every few seconds.
  • Soak damage with a shield.
  • Grant immunities and resistances to other statuses.
  • Show a looping effect on the holder.

Statuses work the same on players and monsters.

There is a second, lighter kind of buff: the Stat Buff skill effect, which changes stats for a while without a status asset. See Plain stat buffs.

#Where statuses are

Game Database > Combat > Statuses lists every status. Create one there (+ New status) or with Create > KitARPG > Status.

In the game:

  • The player's statuses show as icons over the hotbar, with stacks or the shield left, and their seconds left.
  • Monsters show a row of status icons over their nameplate.
  • The boss bar shows the boss's buffs.
  • Hovering a status shows its card: name and duration, what it stops, damage or healing per tick, shield, stacking, how it ends, immunities it grants, and its stat changes.

#Fields

Besides Id, Display Name, Description and Icon:

#Basics

Field What it does Default
Harmful (harmful) A debuff: lands on enemies, cleanses remove it, immunities can block it. Off: a buff that lands on the caster and allies, and dispels remove it. On
Flags (flags) What it stops its holder from doing, or shields it from. Any combination; see Flags. None
Tags (tags) Groups it belongs to, like control, slow, poison or magic. Immunities and resistances name these. Any text; case doesn't matter. Empty
Modifiers (modifiers) Stat changes while it lasts, per stack. A full modifier bundle. Empty

#Over time

Field What it does Default
Tick Seconds (tickSeconds) Seconds between ticks. At least 0.1. 1
Element (element) Element of the damage ticks. Empty uses the default element. None
Damage Per Tick (damagePerTick) Damage per tick and per stack, per status level. Grown by the caster's damage for that element and power, less the holder's resistances. Never critical, at least 1. 0
Heal Per Tick (healPerTick) Health per tick and per stack, per status level. Grown by the caster's heals and heal bonus. 0
Mana Per Tick (manaPerTick) Mana per tick and per stack, per status level. Negative drains mana. 0

#Shield

Field What it does Default
Shield (shield) Damage soaked before health, per status level and per stack. The status ends once the shield is used up. 0

#Stacking

Field What it does Default
Stacking (stacking) What happens when it lands on someone who already has it. See Stacking. Refresh
Max Stacks (maxStacks) Most stacks at once, with Stack. 5

#Ending

Field What it does Default
Ends On Damage (endsOnDamage) Taking damage ends it, as with sleep or freeze. Off
Ends On Action (endsOnAction) Attacking or casting ends it, as with invisibility. Off
Removable (removable) Cleanse and dispel effects can take it off. On

#Protection

Field What it does Default
Grants (grants) Immunities and resistances its holder has while it lasts. When it lands, any status the holder is now immune to comes off. See Immunities and resistances. Empty

#Look

Field What it does Default
Effect (effect) A looping effect on the holder while it lasts: Prefab, Socket (Body, Floor, Head, RightHand, LeftHand), Follow, Lifetime, Tint. It grows with the holder's size. On the body, follows

#Level values

Damage Per Tick, Heal Per Tick, Mana Per Tick and Shield are level values: Base Value at level 1 plus Per Level for each level after it. The status's level is:

  • the skill's rank, when a skill applies it;
  • the item effect's Level, when a consumable applies it.

So Poisoned with 2 damage per tick plus 1 per level does 2 at rank 1 of Poison Arrow and 4 at rank 3.

#Flags

Flag What the holder can't do, or what it gets
Stun Can't move, attack or cast. Cuts the current attack and cast. Plays the Stunned animation.
Root Can't move. Can still attack and cast. Can't dash or leap, but can teleport.
Silence Can't cast skills. Cuts the current cast.
Disarm Can't use basic attacks. Cuts the current attack.
Invisible Monsters lose track of it. Enemy players don't receive it over the network at all, so a modified client can't show it. Allies and the holder still see it.
Invulnerable Takes no damage ("Immune" shows instead) and no harmful statuses.
Unstoppable Stuns, roots, silences and disarms don't take hold, and it can't be pushed or pulled.

Stun, Root, Silence and Disarm are the control flags. While a holder is unstoppable, control flags on it are ignored.

In top-down mode, a stunned or rooted player doesn't walk but keeps the order, and goes on when it ends. Slows change the walking speed as they come and go.

#Stacking

Option What happens when it lands again
Refresh The timer restarts. A shorter one never cuts a longer one: a 1 second stun landing on a 3 second stun keeps the 3 seconds.
Stack Adds the new stacks, up to Max Stacks, and restarts the timer. Modifiers, damage, healing, mana and shield all count per stack.
Ignore Can't land again until it ends.

Two statuses are "the same" when they come from the same status asset. Different statuses never stack with each other.

#Damage, healing and mana over time

Ticks happen every Tick Seconds while the status lasts:

  • Damage: Damage Per Tick × stacks, then the element formula: plus the caster's flat damage for the element, times (1 + caster's power), minus the holder's flat resistance, times (1 − percent resistance, up to the element's limit). Never critical, at least 1. Shields and invulnerability apply.
  • Heal: Heal Per Tick × stacks, then (amount + caster's heals) × (1 + caster's heal bonus).
  • Mana: Mana Per Tick × stacks, added or taken.

The caster's stats are recorded when the status lands, so a poison keeps ticking at full strength after its caster leaves or dies. Kills by damage over time count for the caster.

#Shields

A status with Shield soaks damage before health. With several shields, the oldest soaks first. When nothing gets through, "Absorbed" shows over the holder. A shield status ends when its shield is used up, even with time left. With Stack, each stack adds its shield.

#Immunities and resistances

A status defense (StatusDefense) says which harmful statuses a fighter shrugs off or holds for less time. Classes have one (Status Defense), monsters have one, and a status grants one while it lasts (Grants).

Field What it does Default
Immune To (immuneTo) Statuses with any of these flags never land. None
Immune Tags (immuneTags) Statuses with any of these tags never land. Empty
Immune Statuses (immuneStatuses) These statuses never land. Empty
Resistances (resistances) Statuses that land for less time. Empty
Tag (tag) Any status with this tag. Leave empty to name one status instead. Empty
Status (status) One status, when Tag is empty. None
Percent (percent) Shorter by this whole percent, from 0 to 100. 100 is immune. 0

How it is applied when a harmful status tries to land:

  1. If the holder is invulnerable, it doesn't land.
  2. If the holder is unstoppable and the status has a control flag, it doesn't land.
  3. If the holder's class or monster defense, or any status on the holder that grants a defense, is immune to it (by flag, tag, status, or a 100% resistance), it doesn't land.
  4. Otherwise, the strongest matching resistance across all those defenses shortens its duration: a 40% resistance turns a 5 second stun into 3 seconds.

A refused status shows "Immune" over the holder, at most once a second, so zones don't spam it.

Defenses only stop harmful statuses. Buffs always land.

Examples from the sample:

  • Unbreakable (status): Unstoppable, and grants immunity to the slow tag.
  • Warded (status, from the Antidote): grants immunity to the poison tag. When it lands, a poison already on the holder comes off.
  • Giant Slime (monster): slows last half as long on it.
  • Mushroom King (boss): immune to stuns, roots and silences; slows last half as long and poison 30% less.

#Plain stat buffs

The Stat Buff skill effect gives a buff without a status asset: a modifier bundle for a duration, or a debuff with every number turned negative. Its icon is the skill's icon. It has no flags, ticks, shield or stacking; casting the same skill again replaces it. Cleanse and dispel can remove it. See Stat Buff.

Use a status instead when you want ticks, control, a shield, stacks, tags for immunities, or the same buff from several skills and items.

#Removing statuses

  • Remove Status skill effect: cleanse (debuffs from allies) or dispel (buffs from enemies), filtered by status or flag, a number or all. See Remove Status.
  • Statuses with Removable off never come off this way. The sample's Invulnerable, Protected, Guarded and Enraged can't be removed.
  • All statuses end when their time runs out, when their holder dies, and through Ends On Damage or Ends On Action.

Two statuses are set in Game Database > Settings:

Field What it does Default
Boss Guard Status (bossGuardStatus) Put on a boss while the monsters it called are alive. The sample uses Guarded. None

#Make a status

This example makes Frozen: the holder can't move or attack, takes more water damage while frozen, and breaks free when hit. Warriors resist it.

  1. Open Game Database > Combat > Statuses and press + New status. Type frozen in Id and Frozen in Display Name, and write a Description like "Frozen solid. Breaks when hit." Set an Icon.
  2. Keep Harmful on. In Flags, tick Root and Disarm.
  3. In Tags, add control and cold.
  4. In Modifiers > Resistance Percent, add Water with -0.2: frozen targets take 20% more water damage. Leave Damage Per Tick at 0 (see the warning below).
  5. In Stacking, set Stacking to Refresh.
  6. In Ending, tick Ends On Damage. Keep Removable on so Purify can thaw an ally.
  7. In Look, set Effect to an ice effect prefab on the Body socket, with Follow on.
  8. Put it on a skill: open Frost Nova and replace its Rooted effect with Apply Status: Frozen, 3 seconds.
  9. Make warriors tougher against it: open Classes > Warrior > Status Defense > Resistances, add Tag cold, Percent 50.
  10. Play a mage, cast Frost Nova at a group of monsters: they stop and stop attacking; the first hit on each breaks its ice. The card of Frost Nova shows "Frozen for 3s", "Can't move", "Can't attack", "Ends when hit" and the water resistance change.
Atención

Ends On Damage counts every hit, the status's own damage ticks included. A status with both Ends On Damage and Damage Per Tick ends on its first tick.

#Single player and MMO

Statuses run on the server in both modes. The server keeps the list, runs the ticks, ends statuses and sends every client a light view of each status (id, end time, stacks, shield left) plus the combined flags; clients draw icons and effects from that.

  • Statuses are not saved. They end when the character dies or leaves the map.
  • Invisibility is enforced by the network visibility system in MMO: enemy players' clients don't receive an invisible fighter at all. In single player, an invisible monster is hidden on the host as well.

#Tips

  • Use tags generously. A boss that is immune to control is one line, and covers every stun, root and silence you add later.
  • Keep stuns short (1 to 2 seconds) and give bosses resistances instead of full immunity, so control still feels useful.
  • Use Stack for damage over time players should build up, Refresh for control and buffs.

#Common mistakes

  • A buff doesn't land on an enemy. Statuses with Harmful off land on the caster and allies. Turn Harmful on for debuffs.
  • A status ends at once. The skill or item that applies it has a Duration of 0, or it has Ends On Damage together with damage ticks (see above).
  • An immunity doesn't block a status. The status lacks the tag or flag the defense names. Tags must match (case doesn't matter).
  • A slow of -40 makes characters stand still. Percent stats are fractions: -40% is -0.4.
