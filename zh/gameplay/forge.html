Forge

此页面尚未翻译，以英文显示。

The smith's forge, where players upgrade gear step by step, salvage it into materials and set gems in sockets: the window, every rule and setting, what the server checks and how to set it up.

#What it is

The forge is opened at an NPC with a Forge option. With it a player can:

  • Upgrade a piece of gear one step at a time (+1, +2...). Each step costs gold and materials, has a chance to succeed and a rule for what happens when it fails.
  • Salvage a piece into materials such as runes, one at a time or all plain copies at once.
  • Socket gems into a piece, and take them out for a fee.

It works on pieces in the bag and on worn ones (salvage only takes bag pieces). It is the Forge module: PlayerForge on the player prefab, ForgeOption on an NPC and ForgeWindow in the HUD.

How upgrade rules, sets and sockets are defined as data is in Sets, upgrades and sockets. This page covers how the forge uses them.

#The sample forge

The sample has Garrick, the smith in Town. His options are Forge and a shop, "Buy runes, gems and arrows", with Forge Runes, Protection Scrolls, gems, arrows and bolts.

Item What it is
Forge Rune The upgrade material. What salvaged gear leaves. Stacks to 200, weight 0. Monsters drop them.
Protection Scroll Spent on a failed step, it keeps the piece as it was. Stacks to 20, weight 0.
Ruby Gem: +3 Strength.
Sapphire Gem: +3 Intelligence.
Emerald Gem: +3 Dexterity.
Amethyst Gem: +20 maximum health.

Gems stack to 20 and weigh 0. Most sample gear has one or two sockets.

The sample's Standard Upgrades rules have 10 steps, safe early and risky later:

Step Gold Forge Runes Chance If it fails Bonus added
+1 20 2 100 % Only the cost is lost 6 %
+2 80 4 100 % Only the cost is lost 6 %
+3 180 6 90 % Only the cost is lost 6 %
+4 320 8 80 % Loses a level 6 %
+5 500 10 70 % Loses a level 6 %
+6 720 12 60 % Loses a level 10 %
+7 980 14 50 % Breaks 10 %
+8 1,280 16 40 % Breaks 10 %
+9 1,620 18 30 % Breaks 10 %
+10 2,000 20 20 % Breaks 10 %

A +10 piece has 80 % more on its own bonuses. Salvage gives 3 Forge Runes, plus 2 per rarity above Common, plus 2 per upgrade level, plus 1 for every 10 levels the piece requires. Taking a gem out costs 50 gold.

#Settings

#Module

Turn the forge on or off in KitARPG > Modules (module Forge). Off, the Forge option disappears from NPCs and the server refuses every forge request.

#Upgrade rules

The default rules are the Upgrade Rules (upgradeRules) asset of the Game Database. A piece can use its own. Create one with Create > KitARPG > Items > Upgrade Rules.

Field What it does Default
Steps (steps) One entry per level. The first takes a piece to +1. The number of steps is the highest level. Empty
Protection (protection) An item the player can choose to spend so a failed step keeps the piece as it was. Empty: no protection. Empty
Salvage (salvage) What salvaging or breaking a piece gives. The first line grows with rarity, level and upgrades. Empty
Salvage Per Level (salvagePerLevel) More of the first salvage line for each upgrade level the piece had. 1
Salvage Per Rarity (salvagePerRarity) More of the first salvage line for each rarity above Common (Magic 1, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4). 1
Salvage Levels Per Extra (salvageLevelsPerExtra) One more of the first salvage line for every this many levels the piece requires. 0 turns it off. 10
Unsocket Gold (unsocketGold) Gold to take a gem out of a socket. The gem comes back. 50

Each step:

Field What it does Default
Gold (gold) Gold the step costs. 0
Materials (materials) Items and amounts the step costs, taken from the bag. Empty
Chance (chance) Chance to succeed, in whole percent (0 to 100). 100
On Fail (onFail) Keep: only the cost is lost. LoseLevel: drops one level. Reset: back to +0. Break: the piece is destroyed and leaves its salvage. Keep
Bonus Percent (bonusPercent) Whole percent this step adds to the piece's bonuses and weapon damage. Steps add up. 5

#On each piece of gear

Under Upgrades and sockets on equipment and weapons in the Game Database:

Field What it does Default
Upgradable (upgradable) Off: the piece can't be upgraded and follows no upgrade rules. On
Upgrade (upgrade) Its own upgrade rules. Empty uses the Game Database's. Empty
Sockets (sockets) Gems it can hold. 0
Salvage (salvage) What salvaging or breaking it gives. Empty uses the upgrade rules'. Empty

#On each gem

Gems are GemData items (Create > KitARPG > Items > Gem):

Field What it does Default
Bonuses (bonuses) Stats, attributes, damage or resistances it adds while the piece is worn. None
Fits (fits) Equipment slots it fits in. Empty means any. Empty

#Opening the forge

Talk to an NPC with a Forge option (ForgeOption, label "Forge"). The forge window opens with the bag beside it.

The forge only works while you stand in the NPC's reach (its Talk Range, 3 m by default, plus half a meter), alive, and not trading. Walk away and the window closes. Esc closes it.

#The forge window

Part What it shows
Piece slot The piece being worked on. Empty, it reads "Right click a piece of gear in your bag, or drag one here."
Name The piece's name with its level, like "+3 Knight Helm".
Next step "To +4 80 % chance", the piece's own bonus percent now and after ("Own bonuses 18% → 24%"), each cost with what you have in brackets (red when short), and "If it fails:" with the rule. At the top level it reads "At its best: +10". Pieces that can't be upgraded read "Can't be upgraded."
Salvage line What salvaging it would give, in grey.
Upgrade Tries the next step.
Salvage Breaks the piece into materials. Only for bag pieces.
Protection button "Use Protection Scroll (N)". Click to switch to "Using". Only shows when the rules have a protection item and the next step's failure is worse than Keep.
Sockets One slot per socket, with its gem or empty.
Result What the last request did, green when it went well, red when not. The same text goes to the chat.

#Choosing a piece and gems

Action What it does
Double click a piece in the bag, or Put in the forge in its menu Makes it the piece being worked on.
Drag a piece from the bag or from the character's gear onto the forge Makes it the piece being worked on. Worn pieces are worked on in place.
Double click a gem in the bag, or Put in the forge in its menu Sets it in the first free socket of the current piece.
Drag a gem from the bag onto the forge Same.
Right click a socket with a gem Takes the gem out, for unsocketGold.

If the piece leaves its slot (it broke, was salvaged or moved), the forge goes back to empty.

#Upgrading

  1. Put a piece in the forge.
  2. Check the next step: chance, costs and what a failure does.
  3. If the step can lose the piece or levels and you have a protection item, click the protection button so it reads "Using".
  4. Press Upgrade.

What the server does:

  1. Checks the piece follows upgrade rules ("Name can't be upgraded."), isn't at its top level ("Name is already at its best."), and that you have the gold ("You need 320 Gold.") and every material in the bag ("You need 8 Forge Rune."). The bank doesn't count.
  2. Takes the gold and the materials. They are spent whether the step succeeds or not.
  3. Rolls a whole number from 0 to 99. Below Chance is a success: "Name is now +4."
  4. On a failure, applies On Fail:
    • Keep: "The upgrade failed. Name stays +3."
    • LoseLevel: "The upgrade failed. Name drops to +2."
    • Reset: "The upgrade failed. Name is back to +0."
    • Break: the piece is destroyed and its salvage goes to the bag: "Name broke. You keep 15 Forge Rune." Gems in a broken piece are lost.
  5. With protection chosen and a protection item in the bag, a failure that would be worse than Keep spends one protection item and keeps the piece as it was. A success, or a step whose failure is already Keep, spends nothing.

Worn pieces update the character's stats at once.

#What an upgrade adds

Each step's Bonus Percent adds up. A piece at +N has the sum of the first N steps added to:

  • Its bonuses: the fixed ones and its own random rolls.
  • A weapon's damage rolls, rounded to whole numbers.

Set bonuses and gem bonuses don't grow. The item card shows "Upgraded +3: +18% to its bonuses", and the name shows "+3 Name" in the bag, the item menu and the forge.

The level is saved on the copy (OwnedItem.refine), so it travels with the item through the bank, trades, mail and the market.

#Salvaging

Salvaging destroys a bag piece and gives its materials.

  1. Put a bag piece in the forge.
  2. Check the salvage line.
  3. Press Salvage.

What it gives:

  • The piece's own Salvage list, or the upgrade rules' list when the piece has none.
  • The first line of that list grows by salvagePerRarity per rarity above Common, salvagePerLevel per upgrade level, and one per salvageLevelsPerExtra levels the piece requires. The bonus only applies when the piece follows upgrade rules.
  • Every gem set in the piece comes back too.

The server checks the piece can be salvaged ("Name can't be salvaged." when it has neither its own list nor upgrade rules) and that everything fits in the bag after the piece leaves ("Make room in your bag first."). Then: "You salvaged Name into 7 Forge Rune." Salvages are written to the game log.

#Salvage all

With the forge open, right click a piece in the bag. If the bag has more than one plain copy of that item, the menu shows Salvage all (N). It asks to confirm, then breaks every plain copy at once, up to 100 per request.

  • Plain means not upgraded and with no gems. Upgraded pieces and pieces with gems are left alone; salvage them one at a time.
  • The server adds up the materials of all the copies and checks they fit before breaking anything.
  • "You salvaged 12 Name into 36 Forge Rune."

#Sockets and gems

  • A piece with Sockets above 0 shows that many socket slots in the forge, and a Sockets section on its card.
  • A gem goes into the first free socket. The server checks the piece has sockets ("Name has no sockets."), the gem fits its slot ("Gem doesn't fit Name."), and a socket is free ("Every socket of Name is taken.").
  • Setting a gem is free. The gem leaves the bag.
  • Taking one out costs Unsocket Gold of the piece's upgrade rules (0 for pieces without rules) and needs room in the bag: "Taking it out costs 50 Gold." or "Make room in your bag first."
  • A gem's bonuses count while the piece is worn.

Gems are saved on the copy (OwnedItem.gems), so they travel with the piece.

#Single player and MMO

The forge works the same in both. It runs on the server (the host in single player), and the upgrade level and gems are saved with each copy: in the character's JSON in single player, and in the item row's Extra field in MMO.

#Step by step: set up your own forge

  1. Create the materials (for example a rune) as items in the Game Database.
  2. Create an Upgrade Rules asset. Add steps with gold, materials, chance, failure rule and bonus percent.
  3. Add a protection item if you want one, and the salvage list.
  4. Assign it to the Game Database's Upgrade Rules, or to the pieces that should use it.
  5. Give gear some Sockets and create gems.
  6. Place an NPC and add a Forge Option to it. Add a shop that sells runes, gems and protection items if players should buy them.
  7. Make sure the Forge module is on.

#Tips

  • Let salvage feed upgrades: with NPC selling off (playersSellToNpcs), salvage is how unwanted loot turns into something useful. See Inventory and bank.
  • Make the risky steps rare enough that a Protection Scroll feels worth buying.
  • PlayerForge.ServerUpgraded fires on the server after every upgrade, with the player, the item, whether it worked and the level it ended at (-1 when it broke). Use it for achievements or announcements.

#Common mistakes

  • "You need 8 Forge Rune." with runes in the bank. Materials must be in the bag.
  • The protection button doesn't show. The rules have no protection item, or the next step only loses the cost on failure.
  • The Salvage button is grey for a worn piece. Take it off first; only bag pieces can be salvaged.
  • Salvage all doesn't appear. The forge must be open and the bag must hold more than one plain copy.
  • Upgrading a piece with gems at a Break step. If it breaks, the gems are gone. Take them out first.
  • The forge does nothing during a trade. "Finish the trade first."
