Bosses

Esta página ainda não foi traduzida. Ela aparece em inglês.

A boss is a monster with the Boss rank: it has phases that fire as its health drops, an enrage timer, adds it can hide behind, and a bar at the top of the screen. This page covers every boss field, how a fight runs on the server, and a step by step boss with three phases.

#What a boss is

Any monster becomes a boss when its Rank (rank) is Boss. Everything else about it is a normal monster: stats, attacks, skills, loot, respawn. Read Monsters first.

The Boss rank adds:

  • Phases (phases): things it does once per fight when its health first drops below a percent: shout, put a status on itself, cast a skill, call adds, become untouchable while the adds live.
  • Enrage (enrageSeconds, enrageStatus, enrageShout): after some seconds of fighting it enrages.
  • A full reset when it goes home: health, phases, adds, enrage and every status it put on itself.
  • The boss bar on the HUD, and a boss color on the plate, the bestiary and the world map.

A summoned monster never counts as a boss, even with the Boss rank.

The Elite rank is a lighter step: the name on its plate is gold with an "Elite" tag and the bestiary and map color it, but it has no phases, no enrage and no bar. Make an elite tougher through its own numbers.

#Where it is in the editor

Game Database > World > Monsters, pick the monster. The Rank card holds Rank, Phases, Enrage Seconds, Enrage Status and Enrage Shout. The status used while adds live is a database setting: Game Database > Database > Settings, section Invulnerability, field Boss Guard Status (bossGuardStatus).

#Fields

#On the monster

Field What it does Default
Rank (rank) Boss turns on everything on this page. Normal
Phases (phases) In order of health; each happens once per fight. Empty
Enrage Seconds (enrageSeconds) Seconds into a fight before it enrages; 0 never. The clock starts when it starts fighting someone. 0
Enrage Status (enrageStatus) Status put on itself when it enrages, for the rest of the fight. None
Enrage Shout (enrageShout) Said over its head when it enrages. Empty says nothing. "Enough!"

#On each phase

Field What it does Default
Below Health (belowHealth) Percent of health, 1 to 99. The phase starts the first time health drops below it. 50
Shout (shout) Said over its head, like "Rise, my children!". Also shown on the boss bar. Empty
Status (status) Put on itself, like an enrage or a shield. None
Status Seconds (statusSeconds) How long the status lasts. 0 lasts until the fight ends. 0
Skill (skill) Cast right away, ready or not, at its current target. None
Summon (summon) A monster it calls as adds. None
Summon Count (summonCount) How many adds. Needs Summon. 0
Invulnerable While Summons Live (invulnerableWhileSummonsLive) Takes no damage while the monsters it called are alive. Uses the database's Boss Guard Status. Off

#In the database

Field What it does Default
Boss Guard Status (bossGuardStatus) Put on a boss while the adds of a phase with Invulnerable While Summons Live are alive. It should be an invulnerable status. The sample's is Guarded ("Untouchable while its servants live"). Empty: the phase calls adds but the boss stays hittable. Sample: Guarded

#How a fight runs

All of this happens on the server.

  1. The fight starts when the boss picks a target. The enrage clock starts, and its bar appears on the HUD of nearby players.
  2. Phases are checked as its health drops. Each phase whose Below Health is passed runs once, in list order. A big hit that skips several thresholds runs each of them, one after the other. A phase does its parts in this order: shout, status, adds (and the guard status), skill.
  3. Adds appear around the boss, already aiming at its target, and fight on its side. They are summons: they give no experience or loot, and they go when the boss dies or resets.
  4. The guard comes off by itself the moment the last add of that phase dies.
  5. Enrage fires once when the fight has lasted Enrage Seconds: shout, then the status for the rest of the fight.
  6. The boss dies: phases, enrage and adds reset for the next fight, and it respawns after its Respawn Seconds.
  7. The boss resets if it goes past its Leash Range from home: it forgets its target, heals to full, starts its phases over, sends its adds away and clears every status on itself.

Phases, enrage and the guard can't be removed by players: give the statuses you use there Removable off, as the sample's Enraged and Guarded are.

#The boss bar

BossBar is part of the HUD. It shows the closest boss that is fighting, within 35 m of the player (Range), with its name, health, the buffs it has on itself and its last shout for 4 seconds (Shout Seconds). It hides when no boss is fighting nearby. See HUD and windows.

#Make a boss with phases

This builds a boss like the sample's Mushroom King.

  1. Make the statuses. In Combat > Statuses, make an Enraged status that raises power and attack speed, with Removable off. The sample's gives +50% power, +30% attack speed and +20% move speed. Check that the database's Boss Guard Status is set to an invulnerable status.

  2. Make the adds. Any monster works. The sample uses its normal Mushroom.

  3. Make the boss. Monsters > + New monster. In Combat, set Rank to Boss, a high Health (the King has 1,600) and its damage. In Rewards, a long Respawn seconds (the King has 300) and a loot table with good drops.

  4. Add its skills on its card, under Skills. The King has Royal Stomp and Spore Burst.

  5. Add the phases under Phases:

    Below Health Shout What it does
    75 "Rise, my court!" Summon Mushroom, Summon Count 3, Invulnerable While Summons Live on
    50 "Breathe it in!" Skill Poison Cloud
    25 "You will rot here!" Status Enraged

  6. Set the enrage: Enrage Seconds 150, Enrage Status Enraged, Enrage Shout "Enough of this!".

  7. Protect it from crowd control in Status Defense: the King is immune to stun, root and silence, slows last 50% less and poisons 30% less.

  8. Keep it at home: Wander Radius small (1.5), Leash Range that fits its arena (16).

  9. Place it: in the map scene, add a Monster Spawner with Count 1 and Radius 0 where the boss stands. Then Read from scene on the map's card, so the bestiary lists the boss and its adds. See Maps.

  10. Test it: walk up, or use /spawn mushroom_king and /god. Watch the shouts at 75, 50 and 25%.

Dica

A kill quest for the boss (KillObjective with the boss as Monster and Count 1) and a quest drop on its loot table make a good story ending. See Quests.

#Single player and MMO

The boss logic is the same in both and runs on the server (the host in single player). In MMO, the bar, plate and shouts reach every client that sees the boss. In an instanced map, each copy has its own boss with its own phases, so every party fights it fresh. The sample's Void Herald, at the end of the Sunken Crypt, is a boss in an instanced map.

#For programmers

Monster.RunPhase(BossPhase) is virtual: override it to add your own phase behaviour. Monster.ServerPhaseStarted (monster, phase index) tells other systems when a phase starts. Monster.CallAdds(MonsterData, int) calls adds from your own code. See Overriding kit behaviour and Events.

#Common mistakes

  • The boss is never invulnerable during adds. The database has no Boss Guard Status, or the status isn't invulnerable.
  • No boss bar. Rank isn't Boss, the boss isn't fighting anyone, or the player is more than 35 m away.
  • Phases never fire again. They fire once per fight. They come back when the boss dies or resets.
  • The boss resets mid fight. Players pulled it past Leash Range. Raise it or shape the arena so the fight stays inside.
  • Two phases fire together. Their Below Health values are close and one hit crossed both. Space them out.
