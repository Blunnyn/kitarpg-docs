Currencies

Esta página ainda não foi traduzida. Ela aparece em inglês.

Currencies are money players hold outside the bag: gold, event tokens, honor points, and a premium currency for the in-game shop. This page covers every field of a currency, which one is gold, how players earn and spend currencies, and how each kind is saved in single player and MMO.

#What a currency is

A currency is an amount a character (or, for premium currencies, an account) holds. It takes no bag slot and weighs nothing. Each currency is an asset, so you can add as many as your game needs.

The game database names one of them as gold: the money monsters drop, NPC shops charge by default, the forge, guilds and the market use, and the bank holds. The sample has three currencies:

Currency Id Use
Gold gold Drops, shops, forge, market. No limit.
Event Tokens event_tokens Handed out during events. Limit 10.000.
Gems gems Premium: spent at the in-game shop (P). Not shown in the bag.

#Where currencies are in the editor

Game Database > Items > Currencies lists every currency. Create one there (+ New currency) or with Create > KitARPG > Items > Currency.

Which one is gold is set in Game Database > Settings, field Gold (gold).

#Fields

Field What it does Default
Id (id) Saved with every character that holds some. Changing it loses those balances. From the file name
Display Name (displayName) Name players see in the bag, shops and messages. Empty
Description (description) Shown in the Game Database. Empty
Icon (icon) Shown next to the amount in the bag and in shop prices. None
Max Amount (maxAmount) Most a character can hold. 0 means no limit. 0
Show In Inventory (showInInventory) Listed under the bag, next to gold. On
Premium (premium) Held by the account, not the character, in a ledger only the servers write. Bought with real money or earned in play, and spent at the in-game shop. Off

Gold is always shown in the bag, whatever Show In Inventory says.

#How players get currencies

Source How Page
Monster kills Loot tables drop gold, between a minimum and a maximum. With Auto Gold in Game Settings it goes straight to the purse; otherwise it falls with the items. Loot
Quest rewards A quest's rewards can include gold and any other currency. Quests
Dialogues and NPC options The Give Currency action adds any currency (empty is gold). Conditions and actions
Selling to NPCs Shops pay gold, when selling to NPCs is turned on. NPC shops
The market Sold lots pay gold by mail. Market
GM commands /gold amount [player] and /currency id amount [player]. GM commands

#How players spend currencies

Where How Page
NPC shops Each shop charges in one currency: its Currency field, or gold when empty. A shop that charges Event Tokens is an event vendor. NPC shops
Dialogues and NPC options The Charge Currency action takes an amount and shows it as the option's price. The Currency condition checks a balance. Conditions and actions
Forge Upgrade steps and taking gems out cost gold. Sets, upgrades and sockets
Skill trainers, guilds, market fees Gold. NPCs, Guilds
In-game shop Premium currencies only. Cash shop

#Limits and overflow

  • A currency never goes below 0.
  • With Max Amount set, the balance is capped there.
  • Gold over the limit isn't lost: what doesn't fit in the purse goes to the gold in the player's bank, for loot, mail, guild withdrawals and quest rewards. Taking gold out of the bank stops at what fits in the purse.
  • Other currencies over their limit are capped, and the extra is dropped.

#Premium currencies

A premium currency is the in-game shop's money (the sample's Gems). It works differently from every other currency:

  • It belongs to the account, so every character of the account shares one balance.
  • The balance lives in the Shop service, in a ledger only the servers write (currency_ledger). The map server talks to the Shop service; the client never does.
  • It can be given (a quest reward, the Give Currency action, /currency), and the grant goes to the ledger. It can't be taken by actions or GM commands that take; it is only spent at the in-game shop, where the ledger checks the balance.
  • It is bought with real money through the Shop service's payment setup.
  • There is no in-game shop in single player. Grants of a premium currency there go nowhere.

Tick Premium on a currency to make it the shop's currency, and usually untick Show In Inventory: the shop window shows the balance. Everything about the shop and its service is on Cash shop and Shop service.

#Make a currency

This example adds Honor, earned in quests and spent at a PvP vendor.

  1. Open Game Database > Items > Currencies and press + New currency. Type honor in Id and Honor in Display Name. Set an Icon.
  2. Set Max Amount to 50000 and keep Show In Inventory on.
  3. Give it as a reward: in a quest's rewards, add a currency reward with Honor and an amount. Or add a Give Currency action with Honor to a dialogue line.
  4. Make a shop that charges it: in Game Database > Items > Shops, make a shop, set its Currency to Honor and list the items it sells with their prices. Put a Shop option with that shop on an NPC.
  5. Play: Honor shows under the bag next to gold, and the vendor's prices show in Honor.

#Single player and MMO

What MMO Single player
Gold The character's Gold column. Inside the character's JSON.
Other currencies The character's Currencies column (JSON). Inside the character's JSON.
Premium currencies The Shop service's ledger, per account. Not available.

A balance of a currency that no longer exists in your data is kept as it is, so removing a currency by mistake and adding it back loses nothing. In the game, the owner receives their balances (other players never see them); the server alone changes them.

#Tips

  • Use a separate currency, not gold, for anything you don't want players to trade: currencies can't be traded or mailed, items and gold can.
  • Set a Max Amount on event currencies so players spend them before the next event.

#Common mistakes

  • Gold doesn't drop or shops show no prices. Game Database > Settings > Gold is empty. Set it to your gold currency.
  • A currency doesn't show in the bag. Show In Inventory is off, or the currency isn't in the database's Currencies list.
  • Premium currency grants don't arrive. The map server isn't connected to the Shop service, or you are in single player.
