KitARPG.Modes.TopDown

Esta página ainda não foi traduzida. Ela aparece em inglês.

#Blunnyn.KitARPG.Modes.TopDown

#MoveControls (enum)

How the player walks in top-down: clicking the ground, the keys, or both.

#TopDownCamera (class)

Diablo-style camera that trails the local player. Mouse wheel or a pinch zooms, unless the pointer is over the UI.

#TopDownInput (class)

Client half of click-to-move. Clicking a monster attacks it, clicking loot picks it up, clicking an NPC talks to it, anywhere else walks there; holding the button keeps steering, a few times a second, well under the server's flood limit. Right clicking another player opens the player menu. Skills are aimed at the pointer: the monster or ally under it, or the ground. WASD or the arrows walk relative to the camera, Space attacks the nearest enemy and F picks up or talks to whatever is closest. The player picks click, keys or both in Settings > Controls. With TouchControls, the stick walks, a finger on the world does what a click does, and the Attack and Interact buttons do what Space and F do.

Member Description
public bool playerChooses Adds Movement to Settings > Controls, so each player picks for themselves.
public float keyAttackRange Meters around the player in which Space looks for an enemy.
public float keyInteractRange Meters around the player in which F finds loot or an NPC.
public float keyRepeatSeconds Seconds between direction updates while a key is held.
public int playerMenuButton Mouse button that opens the menu on another player: 0 left, 1 right, 2 middle.
public float playerPickPixels Pixels at 1080p from a player's body at which the pointer still picks them.
