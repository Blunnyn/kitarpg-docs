Trade

Esta página ainda não foi traduzida. Ela aparece em inglês.

Face to face trading of items and gold between two players on the same map: how to start it, the trade window, what the server checks and the settings that change it.

#What it is

A trade is a safe swap between two players standing close to each other. Each side puts bag items and gold on the table, locks the offer, and both accept. The server then swaps everything at once, or nothing.

  • It is the Trade module (PlayerTrade on the player prefab, TradeWindow in the HUD).
  • It needs two players, so it only matters in MMO. In single player there is nobody to trade with.
  • Items keep their own values: random bonuses (rolls), upgrade level and gems travel with the copy.

For selling to players who are not online, use the Market. For sending items far away, use Mail.

#Settings

#Module

Turn trading on or off in KitARPG > Modules (module Trade). Off, the Trade line disappears from the player menu and the server ignores trade requests.

#On the player prefab

The PlayerTrade component on the player prefab:

Field What it does Default
Range (range) Meters between the two players to start a trade. The trade ends at 4 m past it. 8
Max Items (maxItems) Different stacks each side can offer. 12
Request Seconds (requestSeconds) How long a request waits for an answer on the server. 30

The TradeWindow in the HUD has its own Request Seconds (requestSeconds, 30): how long the request panel stays on screen. Keep both the same.

The sample trade window has 12 slots per side. If you raise maxItems, add slots to the window too (mySlots and theirSlots).

#Starting a trade

There are two ways:

  • Player menu: right click another player (in top-down) or their name in the chat, then Trade. See HUD and windows.
  • Chat command: type /trade Name in the chat. See Chat.

The server checks that:

  • The other player is on the same map, within range (8 m), and both of you are alive.
  • Neither of you is already trading.
  • You haven't sent another request in the last 2 seconds. This stops one player from burying another in trade windows.

If something fails you get a chat line: "Name is not here.", "Name is too far away." or "One of you is busy." Otherwise you see "You asked Name to trade."

The other player sees a panel: "Name wants to trade with you." with Accept and Decline. The request expires after 30 seconds. Declining tells you "Name doesn't want to trade."

#The trade window

When the trade opens, both players see a window titled "Trade with Name", and the bag opens beside it.

Part What it shows
Your side Up to 12 slots with what you offer, and a gold field.
Their side Up to 12 slots with what they offer, and their gold.
Status For each side: Choosing, Locked (yellow) or Accepted (green).
Lock / Unlock Locks your offer, or unlocks it.
Trade Accepts. Only active when both sides are locked and you haven't accepted yet.
Cancel Ends the trade for both.

Pointing at any offered item shows its card, so you can check the other side's rolls and upgrade level before you accept.

#Offering items

While the trade is open, the bag's double click and item menu offer the item (the menu line reads Offer):

  • Shift + double click: offers one.
  • Ctrl or Cmd + double click: offers the whole stack.
  • Double click alone on a stack: a dialog asks how many.
  • Offering the same bag slot again changes the amount on the table instead of adding a second entry.
  • Right click one of your offered items to take it back.

The items stay in your bag until the trade goes through. Each entry remembers the exact copy it came from.

#Offering gold

Type an amount in your gold field and leave the field (or press Enter). The server lowers it to what you actually have.

#Lock and accept

  1. Both players choose their items and gold.
  2. Each presses Lock. The status turns yellow.
  3. When both are locked, the Trade button lights up. Each presses it. The status turns green.
  4. When both have accepted, the server swaps.

Any change unlocks both sides. Adding, removing or changing an item or the gold sets both offers back to Choosing and clears both acceptances. Unlocking also clears both acceptances. This stops a last second swap.

#What the server checks

#When offering

  • The item must be in your bag (worn gear has to come off first).
  • Bound copies can't be traded, nor items marked Bound (bound) in the Game Database, nor quest items. The server answers with the item name, for example "Old Letter can't be traded."
  • At most maxItems entries per side.

#When both accept

Everything is checked again against the bags as they are at that moment:

  1. Each side still has the gold it offered. If not: "Not enough gold."
  2. Neither purse would go past the gold cap (maxAmount on the gold currency). If one would: "Name's purse can't hold that much Gold."
  3. Every offered copy is still in its slot, with the same id and at least the amount offered. If not: "The offer changed."
  4. The swap is tried on copies of both bags first. If one side lacks the slots or the weight room for what it receives: "Name has no room for it all."
  5. Only then are the real bags changed, all at once.

The copies that change hands get new unique ids, so the same copy can never be saved twice. Then both players see "Trade complete." in the chat.

#What ends a trade

  • Either player presses Cancel: "Name cancelled the trade."
  • The players move more than range + 4 m apart (12 m by default).
  • Either player dies, leaves the map, changes map or disconnects: "The trade was cancelled."

While a trade is open, the forge refuses to work on that player's items ("Finish the trade first."), so nobody can strip gems from a piece the other side has already seen.

#Records

Every finished trade is written to the game log for both players, with what each gave and got, including gold. GMs read it with /logs. See Game log and reports. Code can listen to PlayerTrade.ServerTraded, which fires on the server with the two characters.

#Single player and MMO

Single player MMO
Available No other players, so no trades. Between any two players on the same map.
Saving Both bags are published at once. Central saves each character's items as rows of item_instances, each with one owner.

#Step by step: a trade between two players

  1. Player A right clicks player B and chooses Trade.
  2. Player B presses Accept.
  3. Player A double clicks a sword in the bag. It appears on A's side.
  4. Player B types 500 in the gold field.
  5. Both press Lock, then Trade.
  6. The sword moves to B's bag and 500 gold to A's purse.

To test this in the editor, use Play with two players (see Play and Servers).

#Tips

  • Check the other side's items by pointing at them: the card shows their rolls and upgrade level.
  • If you change the offer after the other player locked, they have to lock and accept again. Watch the status line.
  • Make items that should never change hands Bound in the Game Database.

#Common mistakes

  • "Name is too far away." The players must be within 8 m to start. Walk closer.
  • The Trade line is missing from the player menu. The Trade module is off.
  • The Trade button stays grey. Both sides must be locked. If one side changed the offer, both were unlocked.
  • "Name has no room for it all." The receiver needs free slots, or weight room in Weight mode.
  • Can't offer worn gear. Take it off first; only bag items can be offered.
