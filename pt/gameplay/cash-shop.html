Cash shop

Esta página ainda não foi traduzida. Ela aparece em inglês.

The cash shop, or in-game shop, sells offers for a premium currency (the sample's Gems) that the account holds, not the character. Players open it from anywhere with P, browse categories and a banner, buy for themselves or send gifts. The Shop service, a separate process, owns the catalog, the balances, every purchase and real-money payments. This page covers what players see, how buying works and what the servers check, and how to set up offers with pictures and banners. It works in MMO only.

#For the player

Press P or click the shop button in the icon bar at the top right. The button shows in online games, on PC and on touch screens, while the in-game shop module is on.

#The window

  • Left side: a search field and the categories. The first, Home, shows every offer with the banner on top. Each other category is one entry, named by the offers' Category.
  • Top bar: sorting (Relevance, Newest, Price: low to high, Price: high to low) and your balance with the currency's icon. A + next to the balance opens the game's top-up web page, when the game has one.
  • Banner: on Home, wide pictures of the offers that have one take turns every 6 seconds, with arrows and dots to move between them. Clicking it opens the offer.
  • Cards: one per offer, with its picture (or its item's icon), a ribbon in the corner, the name and the price with the currency's icon. Ribbons are the offer's badge (like New or Sale), or Limited time for offers with an end date and no badge.

While it loads, the window says "Loading the shop...". If the server has no shop it says "The shop isn't open on this server."; if the service doesn't answer, "The shop can't be reached right now. Try again in a moment."

#An offer's page

Click a card to open the offer's page: its picture, the price, Buy and Gift, what it contains as item slots (hover one for its details), its description, and lines such as "It can be bought once per account."

  • Buy asks "Buy offer for price currency?" and buys on confirm.
  • Gift asks for a character name and sends it to them. Only offers marked giftable can be gifts, and you can't gift yourself.
  • An offer bought up to its per-account limit shows Bought.

#Where bought items go

Bought items and gifts arrive as a letter from the shop, and the map takes them straight into the bag as soon as they arrive. The player reads "subject arrived in your bag.". If the bag and the account bank have no room, the letter waits in the mail and the player is told once. The game setting Shop Auto Take turns this off, so purchases always wait in the mail.

#How buying works

  1. The client asks its map server. The client never talks to the Shop service.
  2. The map checks the offer against its copy of the catalog (on sale, dates, per-account limit, giftable, not gifting yourself) and makes the item copies with their random bonuses.
  3. The map sends the offer id and the copies to the Shop service with its key.
  4. The Shop service charges its own price, never one from the map, checks the copies against the offer and the item catalog, and in one transaction writes the debit to the account's ledger and a letter with the items. One buy at a time.
  5. The map asks Central to refresh the buyer's (or the gift's recipient's) mailbox, and takes the letter into the bag.

Paying and getting the items is one write, so a failure never charges without delivering or delivers without charging. A player waits for one buy to finish before another ("Wait for the last purchase to go through.").

Every buy is written to the game log as shop.premium_buy or shop.premium_gift.

#Balances

Each change to a balance is a row in currency_ledger (account, currency, change, reason, a unique reference). The balance is their sum. The unique reference makes a repeated payment, grant or webhook count once. A refund can take a balance below zero, and the player can't buy until it is back above the price.

#Earning premium currency

Source How
Playing Any reward that gives a premium currency (quest rewards, the Give Currency action) goes to the account through the Shop service. In the sample, the Welcome quest gives 20 gems and the king's quest 100
GMs /currency gems 100 Name, which works for offline characters too. See GM tools
Real money Payment webhooks from Stripe or your own web shop, with the products listed in the catalog. See Shop service

#Setting it up

#1. A premium currency

Tick Premium (premium) on a currency in the Game Database (Currencies). A premium currency is held by the account in a ledger only the servers write. The sample has Gems. If your project has none, the Shop page offers a kit update that adds Gems. See Currencies.

The player prefabs need the PlayerShop component. The kit update Shop on the player prefabs adds it to older projects.

#2. Turn it on and start the service

Open the Shop page of the KitARPG window (KitARPG > Shop):

  1. Turn on the switch under In the game. It turns on the in-game shop module and the shop in the launcher settings.
  2. Under On this computer, press Start the shop. It builds the service if needed and starts it with the launcher in --shop-only mode.

Details of every button are on the Shop page.

#3. Offers

Each offer has:

Field What it does Default
Id (id) Never changes once players bought it. Up to 64 characters, unique Empty
Name (name) Shown on the card and the page Empty
Description (description) Shown on the page Empty
Category (category) One entry on the left side of the shop per category Featured
Icon (icon) Item whose icon the card shows when it has no picture. Empty: the first item's Empty
Currency (currency) The currency id it costs Empty
Price (price) Above 0 0
Items (items) Item ids and amounts it gives. At least one Empty
Starts At, Ends At (startsAt, endsAt) UTC, ISO 8601 (2026-12-01T00:00:00Z). Empty is always Empty
Per Account (perAccount) Times one account can buy it. 0 is no limit 0
Giftable (giftable) Can be sent as a gift On
Hidden (hidden) Kept for history but no longer sold Off
Badge (badge) Ribbon on the card, like New or Sale. Up to 24 characters Empty
Order (order) Lower comes first when sorted by relevance 0
Picture (image) Key of the card and page picture in the game's Shop Art Empty
Banner (banner) Key of a wide picture; offers with one take turns in the banner Empty

#4. Pictures and banners

The catalog is text in the Shop service, so it can't hold sprites. Pictures live in the game, in a Shop Art asset (ShopArt, Create > KitARPG > Shop Art), as a list of keys and sprites. The offer names the key.

Field What it does Default
Shop Art (shopArt), Game Settings > Shop The Shop Art asset the game uses Sample: ShopArt
Pictures (pictures), on the asset Key and sprite pairs Empty

On the Shop page, pick any sprite for an offer's Picture or Banner: the page adds it to Shop Art under the sprite's name and writes that name in the offer.

The sample has 13 card pictures and 3 banners, drawn by Art/Tools/make_shop_art.py from the items' icons.

Atenção

Pictures ship inside the game build. An offer whose key isn't in the players' Shop Art shows the item's icon instead. Build and ship the game before publishing offers that use new pictures.

#Step by step: a limited-time offer with a banner

  1. Draw a card picture and a wide banner and import them as sprites.
  2. On the Shop page, under Offers, add an offer. Give it an id (winter_cloak), a name, a description and the category Seasonal.
  3. Set Currency to gems, a price, and add its items.
  4. Set Ends At to the last moment in UTC, for example 2026-12-31T23:59:59Z. Leave Badge empty to get Limited time, or write Sale.
  5. Pick the card sprite in Picture and the wide sprite in Banner. Give it Order 0 to come first.
  6. Press Save. With the shop running on this computer, the offer is live at once: open the shop in Play to see it.
  7. Build the game client so players have the pictures.
  8. For the live server, fill Online server (address and admin key) and press Publish to server. Publishing is all or nothing: if any offer has a problem, nothing changes and the page lists the problems.

The service reloads its catalog file by itself when it changes, so offers go live without rebuilding the game or restarting servers.

#Game Settings

In KitARPG > Game Settings, section Shop:

Field What it does Default
Shop Art (shopArt) Pictures for cards and the banner, found by the keys offers name Empty (sample: ShopArt)
Shop Top Up Url (shopTopUpUrl) Page where players buy premium currency, opened by the + next to their balance. Empty hides the button Empty
Shop Auto Take (shopAutoTake) Purchases and gifts go straight to the bag when they arrive. Off, or when the bag is full, they wait in the mail On

On the window itself (CashShopWindow in the HUD prefab): Toggle Key (P), Home Name ("Home"), Banner Seconds (6), Badge Color and Sale Badge Color (a badge containing Sale Word, "sale", takes the second color).

#Single player and MMO

There is no cash shop in single player: the button doesn't appear and P does nothing. Premium currency from quests isn't kept in single player.

In MMO the Shop service has to run next to Login and Central. Map servers find it with -shop url -shopKey key, or KITARPG_SHOP_URL and KITARPG_SHOP_KEY. In the editor, Play uses the shop the Shop page started. See Shop service for endpoints, keys, payments and deployment.

The in-game shop module needs the Mail module.

#Security

  • The client only asks its map. The internal and admin APIs of the Shop service stay off the internet; only payment webhooks are public.
  • The Shop service charges from its own catalog and validates every copy, so a modified map or client can't change prices or items.
  • Run one Shop service per database.

See Security.

#Common mistakes

  • No shop button. The game is single player, the in-game shop module is off, or the map server has no Shop service configured.
  • "The shop isn't open on this server." The map server started without -shop or KITARPG_SHOP_URL. With Play & Servers, turn on the shop under In the game.
  • An offer doesn't appear. It is hidden, out of its dates, has no items, or names an item the database lacks. The service logs and skips bad offers; KitARPG > Project Check flags items and currencies the Game Database doesn't have.
  • Cards show icons instead of pictures. The picture keys aren't in the game's Shop Art, or the client build is older than the pictures.
  • Bought items aren't in the bag. The bag and bank were full. They wait in the mail.
